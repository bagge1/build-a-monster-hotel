**The hotel chain (replaces "rebirth = move to a new location", §6.4):**
- 3 locations at launch: Haunted Manor, Frostfang Lodge, **Bayou Spa** (family to design, 8–10 species, his picks).
- Moving to the next location **does not wipe** the hotel: you open a new hotel there, and the old ones keep earning. Each next location is exponentially dearer and earns more.
- The plot shows the hotel you play; a Travel button switches. The others earn in the background at a share he sets, with their own residents on staff (his "yes exactly").
- **All the hotels stand in one world, far apart**, seen from the other plots; the environment changes gradually from one place into the next. The old hotels stay in the world because other players are still in the first area, so it never looks empty.
- **Plots per server: up 8–12** (from 6) so a player is rarely alone. Exact number open.

**Rebirth (now a separate full reset):**
- Requires **5★ (the normal rating) on all three hotels**; the rating is tuned so 5★ is reachable.
- Loses cash, all hotels, the locations unlocked. Keeps residents, Monster Book, cosmetics, Moon Shards, Moon Tree, friendship.
- **Floors:** start at **2**. Rebirths 1, 3, 5, 7… each unlock the right to *buy* one more floor (not free). Cap open: **6 by rebirth, 7 with the pass, or 5 and 6**. The Extra Floor pass adds one on top.
- **Prestige Stars** on even rebirths (2, 4, 6…), separate from the 1–5 rating (rebirth needs normal 5★). After the floor cap, every rebirth gives one. Effect drafted: +income %, slightly rarer guests (values open).
- Moon Shards as today.
- Rebirth 10 takes very long; more content will exist by then.

**Pacing (his targets):** to 5★ at location 1 ~30–60 min, 2 ~60–90, 3 ~90–120 (a rebirth ~3–4.5 h).

**Endless content, his picks:** VIP contracts (generated demands); Expeditions (send residents on timed trips); Decor sets and souvenirs, **rare**; the showcase: like other players' hotels, **the like count displayed**; deeper mutations (stacking, rotating seasonal); residents keep levelling "to some extent" (a cap). **Not** the weekly street league (his doubt: a new street each join).
