# Economy pacing of the hotel chain, 2026-10-21

The pacing model (`tools/balance_model.py`) now plays the whole first run of
the chain as the code built it in commit 89870f2: Haunted Manor, then
Frostfang Lodge, then Bayou Spa, to rebirth-ready (5★ on all three). It
reads every number from `src/` and changes none. Run with
`python tools/balance_model.py --seeds 4 --hours 8 --skip-tutorial`, at
below-normal priority (about 1 s of CPU per simulated hour). 4 seeds per
scenario, so a median is a small sample: read the per-seed columns too.

The model had stopped working before this: the tiles (no more areas), the
removed `Economy.FloorCap` and the new rebirth broke its loader and a guard
(`BuildService.tutorialArea`). The old model assumed a cap of **3 floors**
and areas of 12 usable cells; it now uses `Chain.floorCap(0)` = **2** and the
tile ladder.

## What dominates the misses

1. **The space at a new location costs almost nothing.** Rooms, decor
   and amenities are priced ×20 a location in their rows, matching pay ×20,
   but tiles (`TileCost` $50 × 1.03ⁿ, ×2 upstairs) and floors (`FloorCosts`
   $500 for floor 2) are the same at every location. At Frostfang a
   whole floor of tiles costs what 1–2 minutes of income buy, and at Bayou
   Spa nothing at all. The §6.2 rule of thumb shows it: the median
   meaningful purchase costs 0.16 min of income at Haunted Manor, 0.10 at
   Frostfang and 0.01 at Bayou Spa (2–5 min wanted). A what-if (model only,
   tiles and floors ×20 a location, nothing changed in `src/`): Frostfang
   in the hotel 19 → 32 min, Bayou Spa saving 49 → 67 min and in the hotel
   26 → 35 min, so +62 min and +94 min after the hotel before (both
   targets met) and rebirth-ready 3:12.
2. **A new hotel reaches 4★ within 4–5 minutes of opening.** Its reviews
   start empty, so its first stars come from the categories alone; and
   HotelService's other samples (desk waits, preference shares, requests,
   cleaning times) are made once per session and Travel does not clear
   them, so the new hotel inherits the last hotel's good Service, Comfort
   share and on-time cleaning (3★ five seconds after opening). Then
   what is left is Fun and the reviews: Fun needs 7 amenities and the new
   locations have two amenity types each, one behind a 3★ unlock. This is
   the code's behaviour, mirrored; whether the samples should carry over
   is a question, not a fault I changed. It costs only minutes: with the
   samples reset, the time in the hotel was 18 and 23 min instead of 19
   and 26.
3. **Tier points do not scale with location.** A decor item's points are
   its price × `PointsPerCash`, and the tiers need 100 / 300 points per 2×2
   room at every location, so one $600 Ice Block takes an igloo straight to
   T3. A what-if with the thresholds ×20 a location moved Frostfang in the
   hotel 19 → 24 min and changed the totals little (rebirth-ready 2:21, the
   seeds vary more than this).
4. **The open prices set the saving time.** $250k at Haunted Manor's
   income of about $10–15k a minute is the 30 min saved; $10M at
   Frostfang's $58k a minute at its 5★ would be 174 min, but the player
   keeps buying what pays back before the price is saved and the hotel
   reaches $250–330k a minute, so 49 min. Saving time scales directly with
   the price.
5. **The floor cap of 2 binds only while saving at Frostfang.** By the time
   Bayou Spa is affordable, Frostfang has bought 144 of 144 tiles on floor 1
   and 140 of 144 on floor 2: the hotel is full (30 rooms), so its income
   stops growing from rooms. A what-if with a cap of 3: Bayou Spa saving 49
   → 44 min, rebirth-ready 2:31 (faster, not slower). No 5★ waited on the
   cap: Haunted Manor reached 5★ with 22 rooms on 2 floors.
6. **The background share barely matters for pacing.** Haunted Manor left
   pays $3.2k a minute (25 % of $12.6k), 5 % of Frostfang's income at its
   5★; Frostfang left pays $72k a minute, 4 % of Bayou Spa's. The player's
   income at each step is almost all the hotel played.
7. Guests per bus: mean 5.3 a bus, within 3–8 per player; no location ran
   short of guests in the model (not separately measured).

## What it means, per target

- **5★ at Haunted Manor, 30–60 min:** met with the tutorial (31:35, at the
  fast edge) and just over without it (1:01:37). The early hotel is on target.
- **Frostfang, a further 60–90 min:** 49 min with the tutorial, 64 without.
  Thirty of those minutes are saving the $250k; the hotel itself takes 19,
  because its space is nearly free and its rating starts high.
- **Bayou Spa, a further 90–120 min:** 64–75 min. Saving the $10M takes
  45–49, the hotel 21–26. The same two causes, larger at ×400.
- **Rebirth in 3–4.5 h:** 2:44 with the tutorial, 3:10 without: the whole
  run is 16 min short with the tutorial, inside the band without it.

Values are August's: nothing here is a change. The what-ifs only show which
numbers move the times (tile and floor prices by location, the tier
thresholds, the open prices, the floor cap).

## Assumptions the model makes (no number in the code)

- **The opening policy:** the player opens the next location only once the
  hotel played holds 5★ (a hotel left keeps its rating frozen, and rebirth
  needs 5★ on all three), and the first second cash covers the price plus
  the new location's first room. From 5★ on, it saves: an investment at the
  hotel played is bought only if it pays itself back before the price would
  be saved anyway. It never travels back.
- **Space:** 75 % of a floor's tiles, after the entrance and desk (4 cells on
  floor 1) and the stair (4 cells on each floor it joins), take rooms and
  amenities; the rest is hallway. Cells are counted, shapes not packed.
- **Amenities:** three a floor with rooms, and more until Fun can reach 100
  (7 at 15 each); duplicates when no new type is left.
- One Room Service resident at each hotel (a Room Service species if one
  idles, else the lowest earner); no other staff.
- The player as before: checks in after 4 s, cleans 5 s after check-out,
  collects every 30 s, fulfils 60 % of requests in person.

## What it does NOT simulate

Other players on the street (they split the bus), pitching, Hunter Night,
events, daily rewards, passes (Extra Floor, Auto Collect), the Moon Tree,
Prestige Stars, Book family bonuses, offline earnings, residents' perks,
Front Desk / Housekeeping / Bellhop / Entertainment staff, selling, moving,
windows, hallway decor, travelling back to a hotel left, the time a Travel
takes, the "next location visible as a goal by 15:00" row (a UI moment), and
room shapes on the grid. Nothing was run in Studio: these are the model's
numbers, not a playtest.
