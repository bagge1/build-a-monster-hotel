# Economy pacing with built rooms, 2026-10-21 (b)

The pacing model (`tools/balance_model.py`) no longer simulates tiers. It
plays the chain's first run (Haunted Manor, Frostfang Lodge, Bayou Spa, to
rebirth-ready) with **built rooms** as the code has them since the night of
2026-10-21 (GDD §5.4, A.12): a room is walls, a door, its key furniture and
its cells; its three bars (Size, Furniture, Decor) give exactly the bonuses
of `Rooms.bonuses`, applied as GuestService and ResidentService apply them;
Comfort is `Rooms.comfortPoints`. The chain (three hotels, the open prices,
the 25 % background share) is as in `BALANCE-2026-10-21.md`. It reads every
number from `src/` and changes none; 105 guards stop it if a formula it
mirrors changes shape.

Run: `python tools/balance_model.py --seeds 6 --hours 8 --skip-tutorial`,
at below-normal priority (about 30 s for all 12 runs), on commit 431dfd4.
6 seeds per scenario: a median of six, so read the per-seed columns too.
Nothing was run in Studio: these are the model's numbers, not a playtest.

## What dominates the misses

1. **The later locations are almost all saving.** Frostfang is 60 min of
   saving and 7 in the hotel; Bayou Spa 85 and 10. The open price is the
   gate in every seed (each opened the second it was affordable). Saving
   time is price / income at the hotel before: $250k at Haunted Manor's
   $3–8k a minute, $10M at Frostfang's $60–200k.
2. **5★ comes with a small hotel, so the saving starts from a small
   income.** The bars' satisfaction (+15 Furniture, +15 Decor) lifts every
   stay to 94–100 at once, so the reviews reach 5 within minutes and the
   rating no longer waits for the hotel to grow. This drives both edges:
   with the tutorial, Haunted Manor's 5★ at 26:50 is 3 min under; without
   it, 5★ comes at 57 min with 6 rooms and $569 a minute, and saving the
   $250k then takes 2:01, the 40 minutes over at Frostfang. What-if (in
   memory only, `--set Rooms.FurnitureSatisfaction=0 --set
   Rooms.DecorSatisfaction=0`): 5★ at Haunted Manor 26:50 → 47:17 with the
   tutorial and 56:57 → 1:29:22 without, with 18 rooms instead of 8 at its
   5★; Frostfang's saving 60 → 43 min; rebirth-ready 3:15 → 3:09 and 4:36 →
   4:41.
3. **The tutorial's Vampire Count carries Haunted Manor.** Without the
   tutorial, the first Coffin Suite waits for 3.5★ and its key, the Coffin,
   costs $40,000 (the key pieces cost the old room prices): the first
   Vampire Count checks in at 2:08. With the tutorial's free Coffin it is
   there at 11 min, and the hotel earns 6× as much at 5★.
4. **A new hotel reaches 5★ in 6–10 minutes with 4–7 rooms on one floor.**
   Its reviews start empty (the categories alone count until the first
   check-outs), HotelService's desk-wait, preference and cleaning samples
   carry over from the last hotel (as last night), and Comfort fills fast:
   the Decor target is 300 points per 4 cells at every location, so one
   $600 Ice Block or one $12k Moss Mound fills a room's Decor bar, and the
   Frostfang amenity Cocoa Lounge is keyed by the location-0 Breakfast Buffet
   at $600 instead of its $6,000 Cocoa Bar (`keyFor` lists both). Code
   behaviour, mirrored; whether it is meant is his call.
5. **The bus, then the floor cap, limit the income while saving.** At most
   8 guests a bus every 3 minutes per player: Frostfang holds 22 rooms on
   142 / 140 of its 144 + 144 tiles when Bayou Spa is affordable. Space at
   a later location still costs the same $50 × 1.03ⁿ tiles as at Haunted
   Manor (last night's finding 1, unchanged).
6. **§6.2's rule of thumb is not met.** The median meaningful purchase
   costs 0.25 / 0.19 / 0.15 minutes of income at the three hotels (2–5
   wanted), and the longest gap is 45 min at Frostfang (saving for $10M),
   against "never more than 10".
7. **The room size the player picks matters less.** A policy what-if
   (`--size full`, every new room at its full size): rebirth-ready 3:15 →
   3:25 with the tutorial, 4:36 → 4:45 without; 5★ at Haunted Manor without
   the tutorial 57 → 48 min.

Values are August's: nothing here is a change. The what-ifs ran in memory
only and are labelled as such.

## Assumptions the model makes (no number in the code)

- **Building a room:** in one go, walls, door, type and the cheapest key
  piece placeable at the location, so it is ready at once. Its walls are
  75 % of the perimeter of the most compact rectangle of its cells, in half
  edges (the rest is the facade or a neighbour's wall); one is its door.
- **Size:** a new room at its minimum or its full size, whichever pays
  back better, its cells charged at the marginal tile price; the score
  leaves out the longer stay. Rooms are never enlarged, moved or retyped.
- **Furnishing:** furniture and decor on at most half of a room's squares;
  up to three pieces chosen for its guests' preferences (bought when ≤ 90 s
  of income); each bar filled with the pieces still missing (the cheapest
  piece worth what is missing, else the dearest) when the fill costs ≤ 2
  minutes of income, or sooner when it pays back best. Amenities get their
  key piece only.
- **Space:** 75 % of a floor's tiles, after the entrance and desk (4 cells)
  and the stair (4 cells a floor it joins), hold rooms and amenities; a new
  room must leave space, on the two floors fully bought, for the amenities
  Fun still lacks (without this, rooms filled both floors first and two
  seeds in each scenario never reached 5★ for want of Fun).
- **The start room** is 4 cells (Constants.Rooms.Start's L): Size bar 0,
  Furniture 0.5 (its key piece is half the target at every location).
- As last night: the opening policy (save from 5★, open the second the
  price is there), 7 amenities for Fun, one Room Service at each hotel,
  check-in after 4 s, cleaning 5 s after check-out, collecting every 30 s,
  60 % of requests in person.

## What it does NOT simulate

Other players on the street (they split the bus), pitching, Hunter Night,
events, daily rewards, passes, the Moon Tree, Prestige Stars, Book family
bonuses, offline earnings, residents' perks and levels, Front Desk /
Housekeeping / Bellhop / Entertainment staff, selling, enlarging, moving or
retyping rooms, hallway decor and furniture, floor finishes, windows,
travelling back to a hotel left, the time a Travel takes, the walk to the
key furniture, room shapes and packing (cells are counted; walls are priced
by the assumption above), and the size and look mutation stack another
session is building (`Mutations.luau`, uncommitted at the time of the run:
the model rolled the committed single mutation, and it will stop at load
once that change lands, until its roll is mirrored).
