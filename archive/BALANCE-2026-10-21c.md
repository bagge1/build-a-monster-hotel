# Expeditions against the offline cap, 2026-10-21 (c)

GDD A.11.7 builds Expeditions (A.11.2) only once `tools/balance_model.py` has
checked their pay against the offline cap: the review found the best three on
an 8 h trip at every logout strictly better than anything else. This is that
check, on the draft's **revised** rules (the scrutiny lines of 2026-10-21) and
its PLACEHOLDER numbers. It measures; nothing in `src/` or the GDD is changed.

Run: `python tools/balance_model.py --seeds 6 --hours 8 --expeditions`, on
commit 49d7676 (about 25 s). The new flag is off by default and the normal run
is byte for byte what it was (checked against the previous model, 3 seeds,
both scenarios). Nothing was run in Studio: Expeditions are not built.

## What the what-if plays

- **The run** is the normal first run (with the tutorial); the flag only
  records the Wing (residents not on staff, their rates) and the income every
  minute, and each happy stay of a Rare-or-better guest.
- **The players** log in n times a day for s minutes, evenly spaced, so each
  time they are away H = 24 h / n − s:

  | Player | Sessions | Away each time |
  |---|---|---|
  | Daily 20 min (the 15–20 min player, upper end) | 1 × 20 min | 23:40 |
  | 2 h a day, evening and morning | 2 × 1 h | 11:00 |
  | 2 h a day, four visits | 4 × 30 min | 5:30 |
  | 2 h a day, checks in often | 8 × 15 min | 2:45 |

- **(a) Staying:** every Wing resident earns its rate while the player plays
  and 50 % of it while away, to the 2 h cap from the logout (`Economy.offline`,
  `ResidentService.welcomeBack`; Night Manager's 8 h in one column).
- **(b) Trips:** the three best earners leave on a trip of 30 min, 2 h or 8 h
  at every logout when the board is free ("logout"; "busy" also resends at
  once when a trip ends during play). The trip pays **1.5 × what the party
  would have earned by staying over the trip's own time, counted as staying
  counts** (Wing rate while playing, offline rate while away, nothing past the
  cap from that logout). The party is back in the Wing at the trip's end and
  earns by staying's rules after it.
- **Finds:** 5 / 15 / 35 % a trip by length, among the location's unfound
  pieces (so each find is new), the guarantee read as the 10th trip of 2 h+ in
  a row without a find; 5 pieces a location (A.11.3: one set each at launch).
  A.11.3's happy-stay roll (2 % per happy stay of a Rare-or-better guest,
  guaranteed after 40) at the rate this run measured at Haunted Manor, 0.19 a
  minute of play.

The Wing it works on (medians of six seeds):

| Stage | Location | Wing residents | Wing rate/min | Best 3/min | Income/min while playing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 min of play | Haunted Manor | 5 | $131 | $98 (75 %) | $5.0k |
| 60 min | Haunted Manor | 5 | $465 | $360 (77 %) | $7.6k |
| 120 min | Frostfang Lodge | 4 | $5.2k | $4.3k (83 %) | $185k |
| Rebirth-ready (3:02) | Bayou Spa | 3 | $20.9k | $20.9k (100 %) | $160k |

By rebirth-ready one resident works Room Service at each of the three hotels,
so the Wing of 6 holds 3: the "best three" are the whole Wing.

## 1. Trips still strictly dominate staying

What the party brings home with trips over what it brings by staying, the same
at every stage (1.00 = equal; Pay 1.5, no matching resident):

| Player | Policy | 30 min trips | 2 h | 8 h | 8 h, Night Manager | Party XP a day: stay → 8 h trips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily 20 min | logout | 1.09 | 1.38 | 1.38 | 1.46 | 20 → 180 |
| Daily 20 min | busy | 1.16 | 1.44 | 1.50 | 1.48 | 20 → 160 |
| 2 × 1 h | logout | 1.06 | 1.25 | 1.25 | 1.40 | 120 → 440 |
| 4 × 30 min | logout | 1.08 | 1.33 | 1.42 | 1.33 | 120 → 380 |
| 8 × 15 min | logout | 1.10 | 1.40 | 1.47 | 1.44 | 120 → 467 |

**Every cell is above 1.00, for every player, length and policy: (b) strictly
dominates (a).** The trip pays 1.5 × the staying it replaces and costs
nothing, and the find and the XP come on top. The revision fixed the **size**
of the edge, not its sign: the review's 8 h trip was 12 rate-hours against 1
(1.5 × uncapped Wing earnings); the revised one is 1.5 against 1 on the
offline part, and 1.25–1.50 counting the whole day.

In cash a day (medians; Δ = (b) − (a), and its share of the whole day's cash
in (a), play income plus offline):

| Player | Stage | Play income/day | Offline (a)/day | Δ, 2 h trips | Δ, 8 h trips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily 20 min | 60 min | $152k | $27.9k | $10.8k (+5.8 %) | $10.8k (+5.8 %) |
| Daily 20 min | rebirth-ready | $3.2M | $1.3M | $626k (+13.0 %) | $626k (+13.0 %) |
| 2 × 1 h | 60 min | $909k | $55.8k | $21.6k (+2.1 %) | $21.6k (+2.1 %) |
| 2 × 1 h | rebirth-ready | $19.3M | $2.5M | $1.3M (+5.3 %) | $1.3M (+5.3 %) |
| 4 × 30 min | 60 min | $909k | $112k | $43.2k (+4.1 %) | $54.0k (+5.1 %) |
| 4 × 30 min | rebirth-ready | $19.3M | $5.0M | $2.5M (+9.5 %) | $3.1M (+11.9 %) |
| 8 × 15 min | rebirth-ready | $19.3M | $10.0M | $5.0M (+15.9 %) | $5.8M (+18.6 %) |

The edge is 2–19 % of a day's cash, largest late in the run (the Wing is then a
bigger share of income) and for the player who logs out most often.

Three things make it larger:

- **The +20 % a matching resident, if it is on the reward** (the draft does not
  say on what): three matching make the trip 1.5 × 1.6 = 2.4 × staying. The
  daily player's Δ at rebirth-ready goes $626k → $1.8M a day.
- **The offline till as the code stands (a leak).** `welcomeBack` pays
  `rate(at join) × min(away, cap)`, and `ResidentService.rate` counts every
  resident whose `campUntil` has passed. A.11.2 builds the away state on
  `campUntil`, so a party back before the login is paid the **whole absence's
  offline till on top of the trip**: the 8 h trip rises to 2.12 × staying for
  the daily player (Δ +39 % of the day's cash at rebirth-ready) and 1.71–2.00
  for the 2 h players. Closing it is an implementation matter, not a value: a
  resident's offline time counts only while it was in the Wing. (The Hunter
  Camp has the same leak today, for one resident whose 24 h ends while its
  owner is away.)
- **XP.** A Wing resident earns no XP offline (A.11.6: at work while the player
  plays); a trip earns 20 an hour away. The daily player's party gets 180 XP a
  day instead of 20: the 900 XP to A.11.6's cap in about 5 days instead of 45.
  For the daily player this edge is larger than the cash.

The cap also makes the 8 h trip the only sensible length for a player away
more than 8 h: it pays exactly what a 2 h trip pays (the cap stops both at
2 h) with 35 % instead of 15 %. The lengths are a choice only for a player who
comes back within 8 h.

The review's "30 pieces in days" is gone: the 15 launch pieces take a daily
player 41 days and a 2 h player 10–21. Trips matter most to the daily player
(the happy-stay roll alone takes 36 days for the first five, trips 12); for a
2 h player stays alone already find five in 6 days, and trips halve it.
30 min trips are near useless for finds (5 %, and they do not count toward the
guarantee): the daily player needs 92 days for five.

## 3. What would make it a real choice

These are findings; the values are August's.

- **Pay below 1.** At Pay 1.0 a trip pays exactly what staying pays, so the
  find and the XP are free: still never worse, a trip at every logout is
  always right. Only below 1 does a trip give up cash for its find and XP.
  What a find then costs, in cash given up per find expected (the daily player
  on 8 h trips; the 4 × 30 min player on 2 h trips):

  | Pay | Daily 20 min, at 60 min | … at rebirth-ready | 4 × 30 min, at 60 min | … at rebirth-ready |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1.5 (draft) | gains $10.8k a day | gains $626k a day | gains $43.2k a day | gains $2.5M a day |
  | 1.0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
  | 0.75 | $15.4k a find (2.0 min of play income) | $894k (5.6 min) | $36.0k (4.8 min) | $2.1M (13 min) |
  | 0.5 | $30.9k (4.1 min) | $1.8M (11 min) | $72.0k (9.5 min) | $4.2M (26 min) |

  For scale: a shop decor piece costs $250 median / $60k dearest at Haunted
  Manor, $3k / $150k at Frostfang, $30k / $200k at Bayou Spa, and a set piece
  is worth a decor piece of twice its listed price (A.11.3).
- **Who goes, once Pay is below 1.** The find does not depend on who goes, so
  the cheapest party is the three lowest earners: at 60 min they cost about
  half (daily player, Pay 0.75: $8.2k a find instead of $15.4k). "Best for
  this trip" fills by matching, then level, then **rarity**, which at Pay < 1
  picks the dearest party. At rebirth-ready there is no choice either way: the
  Wing holds only the three.
- **The trip's XP.** A trip sent at logout replaces only offline time, where
  the Wing earns no XP, so any XP an hour away is free. For XP to be part of
  the trade it would count as staying counts (XP only for the part of a trip
  the player plays), or be small enough not to matter (at 20 an hour it is
  9 × the daily player's Wing XP).
- **The matching bonus on the find's chance, not the reward.** On the reward it
  makes a trip 2.4 × staying; on the chance it speeds the finds (12 → 7.3 days
  for the daily player) without touching cash.
- **The offline leak closed** (not a value): otherwise a trip that ends before
  the login pays the reward and the whole offline till as well.

## What is NOT simulated

Residents' levels (both sides scale alike), perks lost while the party is away
during play (perks are not in the model), the Moon Tree's offline nodes,
Night Manager beyond its one column, a second trip slot (Moon Tree), the
value of a find beyond its shop price and the set bonus, rebirth or Travel
mid-trip, income multipliers (both sides alike), other players, notification
timing, and a player who does not play to a schedule. The stages are moments
of the continuous model's run; a daily player reaches them over days (60 min
of play on day 3, rebirth-ready on about day 10). The happy-stay rate is the
model's continuous player at Haunted Manor, applied to the first location's
pieces only.
