# Haunted Manor monsters (Location 1), first drafts

Built 2026-10-19 by an unattended night session, for August's review: every
look here is a draft he judges. Rebuilt by `tools/build_monsters_l1.py` on
`tools/monster_kit.py` (headless Blender 5.2, ~10 s):

```
blender -b --python-exit-code 1 -P tools/build_monsters_l1.py [-- norender] [only Slime,Ghost]
```

**The look in one line:** chunky, rounded "cozy creepy" toys at the 5-stud
person's scale, each in its Species.luau colour, with big glowing eyes that
have a dark pupil and a white glint, rosy cheeks on the soft ones, and nothing
sharp or gory (GDD §12). The sheet is `review/sheet.png` (Workbench, not in
git): a grey 5-stud person at the start of each row.

## What a model is made of

- **Frame.** Standing on the floor, facing Roblox −Z, origin between the feet
  (under the body for the floaters, Ghost and GrandBanshee). Blender −Y is the
  front, +X the monster's left; Roblox (x, y, z) = Blender (−x, z, y).
- **One part per colour, no textures.** `Body` is one mesh in Species.luau's
  `look.colour`; `Skin`, `Trim`, `Dark`, `Light` are the other flat colours.
  No `SurfaceAppearance`, so a mutation's `Color` and `Material` land
  (MonsterKit tints every part but `Root` and `Glow_*`).
- **Rigged** (2026-10-23, LAUNCH.md #6): one armature per body shape, every
  visible part skinned to it (MODELS.md, "The rig"); the six clips are code
  (`MonsterMotion.luau`), no `Animations` folder. `review/pose.png` shows all
  ten in a test pose (every part following its bone). The Skeleton was the
  prototype, proved in Studio (`blender/monsters/rig_proto/README.md`); the
  Grand Banshee's raised arm has its own landmarks (`hand_l`).
- **Markers.** `Nameplate` (1 stud over the head, ~0.9 for the ghost) and
  `Hand` (the right hand; Slime's right side, Ghost's right nub) arrive as
  0.2-stud parts; `import_settings.luau` turns them into Attachments under
  `Root` and deletes the parts.

## Importing (skill `import`)

Staged in `blender/_import/monsters_l1_2026-10-19/` (the ten FBX). After
August imports them into Workspace, run `import_settings.luau` (this folder,
generated by the build) in the Edit datamodel, then read back in a separate
call. It sets what an FBX cannot carry:

- `Root` as `PrimaryPart`, invisible, `PivotOffset` on the floor (cancels the
  importer's −90° X pivot, DEVNOTES "The importer bakes each part's rotation")
- `Nameplate` and `Hand` as Attachments under `Root` (GuestRenderer reads
  `PrimaryPart.Nameplate`), marker parts deleted
- every part's `Color` and `Material` (Neon for `Glow_*`) from the manifest,
  any imported `SurfaceAppearance` removed
- `Box` collision, `CanCollide`/`CanQuery`/`CanTouch` off and anchored on every
  mesh (`collision_precise` is empty: nothing walks into a monster)
- `LOD_Proxy` hidden

Then save each one as `assets/Monsters/<SpeciesId>.rbxm` (`tools/save_imports.py`).
`default.project.json` does not map `assets/Monsters` to
`ReplicatedStorage.Monsters` yet (2026-10-19): that mapping comes first, and a
project-file change means August restarts `rojo serve`.

## Validated, and not

**Validated in Blender** (`finish()` at build, `validate()` on the re-imported
FBX, `validation.json`): the part names and the contract's names, nothing
outside the allowed names, triangle counts and budgets (4,000 + 300, the
per-mesh cap), one material and one UV layer per part, closed geometry, no
degenerate faces, no coplanar faces between any two pieces, height within
10 % of Species.luau, nothing under the floor and the walkers standing on it,
the origin between the feet or under the body, the eyes in front and
`Glow_Eyes_1` on the left, the Nameplate over the head, the Hand on the model,
the Root 2 × h × 2 with its bottom at 0, the markers where the manifest says.
`finish()` was proven to refuse a coplanar pair, a model too tall, eyes facing
backwards, a missing Hand and a part name outside the contract.

**Not validated:** anything in Studio. Not imported, not seen at the game's
camera, not seen at Tiny 0.5 / Giant 1.5 scale, not seen with a mutation.
Whether the importer carries a material's plain colour into `MeshPart.Color`
is unknown, so `import_settings.luau` sets every colour. The axis conversion
and the marker positions are as Sector Omega's kit found them, not re-checked
in this game. `import_settings.luau` parses but has not been run.
