# August's taste, and how to read his pictures

He reviews by eye, in renders and in Studio, and every rule below cost a
review round when it was missed: each was a rejection. Carried over from
Sector Omega (2026-09-17 to 2026-10-18), where these held across ships,
weapons and stations; the ship-specific ones stayed there
(`D:\Roblox\sector-omega\.claude\skills\modelling\taste.md`).

**A new no from him goes in here**, in his words where they say it best, with
the date and the model, in the same commit as the work that drew it. A ruling
that concerns only one model goes in that set's `README.md` instead.

## Reading his references

- Copy the picture into the set (`review/reference.png`) first: his
  inspiration folder gets cleared.
- A 2D flat-shaded sketch beside his picture at the same angles, plus the
  orthographic views, was approved first time when he asked for a sketch
  before modelling.
- An ambiguous view (side or top? which end is the front?) → ask.
- A marker dot he draws sits on a face of the part it is drawn on, and points
  the way he says, not out of the nearest free surface. His colour legend
  differs per picture: read his annotation.
- MS Paint sketches: make them symmetric, clean lines.
- An orthographic set: match features across views before giving a part a
  height. Read outlines off the pixels into tables, and compare the model's
  views with his (overlap ≥ 0.94 was reachable).
- A perspective picture: fit the camera, never eyeball. When the fit fails,
  render the model at the picture's angle beside it and iterate. If only one
  of a symmetric pair fits, the original is asymmetric: show him mirrored
  variants and let him pick.
- Check the cross-section early, from the front and the rear, not only plan
  and side.
- Check a new silhouette against famous ones (film ships, well-known designs)
  before proposing it.
- A real-world reference he names is the design idea, not something to copy.
- **The picture is a guide; his feedback is the authority.**
- Names are working names: he names later, just rename the files.

## His taste

- **No clutter**: no thin raised seam strips, no raised plates on smooth
  surfaces, no details crowding a feature or each other (a hatch beside a slot
  read as a "random dot").
- **No texture grid**: a lattice of panel seams printed over every face looks
  "Roblox-like" ("rutnät är inte snygga"). A clean finish; stripes, bands and
  detail where the design puts them. A deliberate plate pattern on a walked
  floor is fine. No scratches or grime on anything he will look at closely:
  they read as "low-res weird streaks".
- **Continuous surfaces over separate blocks**: one shape runs on into what it
  carries. A joining member (pylon, strut) is an oval section flaring into
  what it joins, never a flat slab sticking out.
- **Things grow out of the body, never stuck on**: a cab, a canopy, a housing
  is lifted out of the body's own surface. A mechanism standing out of its
  housing reads as stuck on; it lies in a groove or recess.
- **Mounts flush**: no raised pads or shelves under things that are mounted.
  On a curved side, a shallow pocket walled in the body's colour.
- **Plain, single-idea shapes bore him**: layered, busy silhouettes are what
  he asks for ("inga enkla tråkiga", 2026-10-03).
- **Real thickness**: a lip or rim is about a quarter of its opening thick;
  thin washers read "paper thin".
- **No flat block ends**: ease the body in round what it ends in, "not fully
  round, not fully angular".
- A colour change needs a separator (he likes a swooping metallic band), not an
  abrupt edge.
- "Smaller" on a part something plugs into means slimmer, never shorter than
  what it holds.
- A surface pattern must not read as cracks: narrow, soft seams in a colour
  near the cells'.
- Anything that retracts closes its bay flush.
- His palette in Sector Omega leaned **white, black and gold with violet and
  blue lights**; ask before assuming it carries to a new game.
