# Classic and Evergreen Roblox Tycoon Landscape (2008-2024): Hall of Fame, Sub-genres, Longevity, Evolution

Data snapshot date for all live stats in these notes: **18 October 2026**, unless a different date is stated.

## Q1. Which Roblox tycoon games have the highest lifetime visits and the longest active lifespans? (Hall of fame, 40+ titles)

### Takeaway
Seven pre-2023 tycoons have passed 1.3 billion visits: Car Dealership Tycoon (2.54B), Super Hero Tycoon (2.49B), Islands (2.36B, a sandbox hybrid), Restaurant Tycoon 2 (1.65B), Theme Park Tycoon 2 (1.62B), Mega Mansion Tycoon (1.37B) and Lumber Tycoon 2 (1.34B). Elemental Powers Tycoon, War Tycoon and Military Tycoon are each just over 1.0B. Raw visits and lasting health are different things, though. Only a small group still draws thousands of concurrent players years after launch: War Tycoon (13.1k), Car Dealership Tycoon (10.0k), Theme Park Tycoon 2 (5.9k) and Lumber Tycoon 2 (5.1k). Most of the 2016-2021 dropper hits (Super Hero Tycoon, Clone Tycoon 2, Pizza Factory Tycoon) and the 2020-2021 business sims (Mall Tycoon, Airport Tycoon!, Tropical Resort Tycoon) have lost 95-99% of their peak CCU.

### Cited Findings

#### Data method and caveats (read first)
- **Visits, favorites, current CCU ("playing"), created and updated dates** come from Roblox's public Games API (`games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=...`). The universe IDs were resolved through Roblox's omni-search API, and everything was pulled on 18 Oct 2026. Every row in the table below links its own API URL. "Created" is the date the place or universe was created, which is **not always the public launch date**. Lumber Tycoon 2's place dates from 2009, but development started in 2015 ([LT2 wiki](https://lumber-tycoon-2.fandom.com/wiki/Lumber_Tycoon_2)).
- **Like % = upvotes / (upvotes + downvotes)**, using vote counts from the Roblox omni-search API on 18 Oct 2026. Spot checks matched Rolimons' "Rating" to 0.1 pt. For example, TPT2 is 89.0% on both ([Rolimons TPT2](https://www.rolimons.com/game/69184822)).
- **Peak CCU, "Average Playtime" and recent 30-day peaks** come from Rolimons game pages fetched on 18 Oct 2026 (one link per game in the table). **Important caveat:** Rolimons' "All-Time" peak covers only Rolimons' own tracking window. The earliest all-time-peak dates it shows for any game here are 1 Jan 2020 ([Miner's Haven](https://www.rolimons.com/game/258258996)) and Mar-May 2020. Peaks from 2015-2019 are therefore probably **not captured** (e.g. LT2 in 2015-16, TPT2 and Super Hero Tycoon in 2017-18). Treat these as "peak since roughly 2020". For small games, Rolimons seems to have started tracking only recently. 2 Player Gun Factory Tycoon shows an "all-time" peak of 366 in Oct 2025 ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/161766693)).
- **RoMonitor Stats** pages are rendered client-side and returned no data to the fetch tool ([RoMonitor CDT](https://romonitorstats.com/experience/1554960397/)). I used no RoMonitor figures.
- **Status** labels are my own classification from the 18 Oct 2026 snapshot. **Alive** = 1,000+ CCU and updated in 2026. **Declining** = 100-999 CCU. **Dormant** = under 100 CCU or no updates for 12+ months.

#### Per-game profiles: core loop, standout features, monetization, why it worked or faded
- **Car Dealership Tycoon (Foxzie).** Officially released 12 May 2018. Players buy dealership parts with cash by walking over buttons and upgrade car platforms. Cars displayed on 9 platforms earn cash per second, and driving earns cash per mile. Other features: 8 race types (circuit, drag, drift, endurance, mountain, police, rally, street), a trading hub, challenges, daily rewards, seasons, houses, police/fire jobs, and deep car customization (paint, wraps, body kits, engine/turbo/brake upgrades) ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Foxzie/Car_Dealership_Tycoon)).
  - Licensing: Zenvo became the first licensed brand on 2 Nov 2024, and a Monster Jam collaboration ran on 28 Oct 2022 and has returned every Halloween since ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Foxzie/Car_Dealership_Tycoon)).
  - The current description calls it "Roblox's #1 driving game" with "600+ cars" ([Rolimons description, 18 Oct 2026](https://www.rolimons.com/game/1554960397)).
  - Monetization: cars sold for Robux and loot-style "Collector Kits" with stated drop odds. Example: a Foxzie-edition VW at 10% from a Collector Kit or 15% from a Deluxe Kit, or 949 Robux during a sale ([CDT wiki](https://car-dealership-tycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Volkswagen_I.D._R_Pikes_Peak_%22Foxzie_Edition%22_(2018))).
  - Updated 17 Oct 2026 ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=605887098)). One aggregator says Foxzie updates "almost weekly"; that comes from a search summary and is unverified ([search result: stealthygaming](https://stealthygaming.com/play-car-dealership-tycoon-on-roblox/)).
- **Super Hero Tycoon (Super Heroes™).** A basic dropper/upgrader/conveyor tycoon with 10 hero-themed bases (Ant-Man, Batman, Black Panther, Green Lantern, Iron Man, Spider-Man, Superman, Flash, Hulk, Thor). Players unlock gear to fight other players, can buy laser doors that kill intruders, and collect parachuted cash crates through a roulette ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Heroes%E2%84%A2/Super_Hero_Tycoon)). The wiki says its popularity "inspired thousands of other games with near-similar thumbnails", often linked to deceptive-advertising scams ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Heroes%E2%84%A2/Super_Hero_Tycoon)). It holds 15.0M favorites, the most of any tycoon in this set, but only 1,180 CCU now ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=228573408)).
- **Islands (Easy.gg).** Launched as "Skyblox/Sky Block", inspired by Minecraft SkyBlock. Players expand an island, gather, craft, farm, fish, raise animals, fight mob islands, automate with totems, conveyors and factories, and trade ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Easy.gg/Islands)). ## Q2. What are the recognisable sub-genres, and which have the best track record?

### Takeaway
The Roblox wiki says the core Roblox "tycoon" is closer to an **idle game** than to a business simulation: claim a plot, buy droppers, collect cash, press buttons to unlock more. Over time it has split into dropper/button tycoons, building/creative management sims, combat/war tycoons, two-player co-op tycoons, sandbox/automation tycoons, collection hybrids (cars, mansions) and fad hybrids (RNG). Classic droppers piled up the most raw visits during the 2016-2018 front-page era but kept few players. **Management/creative sims and combat/war tycoons have the best long-term track record**, and Car Dealership Tycoon's collection and driving hybrid is the single strongest performer.

### Cited Findings
- **Genre definition:** players own a base, usually a business, and use "droppers" to earn cash spent on upgrades. "Despite the name, it is usually reminiscent of idle genre, not the business simulation genre". These games "often have products available for purchase with Robux" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)).
- **Classic loop:** claim a base by walking through a door, get a free dropper, click to make bricks that ride a conveyor to a neon collector (one dollar per brick), step on a collector button to bank the cash, and keep unlocking more buttons. Purchases cover droppers, upgraders that raise brick value, infrastructure, decoration and weapons for combat between base owners ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)). Mining and lumber tycoons need manual tool work, and some tycoons pay out cash automatically ([same](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)).
- **Rebirth** (resetting progress for permanent advantages, like New Game+) is a standard extension. **Completion** is buying the last upgrade, "usually a very expensive statue or object", sometimes with a badge; after that, players "cannot continue building their base" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)).
- **Two-player tycoons** are "a newer type of tycoon which allows two users to collaborate on completing the same tycoon base" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)).
- **Sandbox tycoons:** the first was Dued1's Your Store Tycoon. "Starting in 2016, some modern tycoons have begun to diverge from the old model, by becoming more like simulators designed around customizability". The wiki's examples are Restaurant Tycoon 2, Retail Tycoon and Theme Park Tycoon 2 ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)).
- **War tycoons:** "Since 2021, war tycoons such as Military Tycoon, in which troops are sent to fight at the center of the map have also gained increased traction" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)).
- **Track record by sub-genre** (visits and CCU from the [Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=228573408,3754482795,3079618996,156428258,97314177,110988953,1214576306,31970568,2471084,2092410051,1526814825,2788648141,21449357,605887098,3191268194,4518160318,4925331346,105689481,2566300911,3448264866,1659645941,6026836726,1002043129), 18 Oct 2026; peaks from the Rolimons pages linked in Q1):
  - *Classic dropper/button (often themed or PvP-lite):* Super Hero Tycoon 2.49B / 1,180 CCU; Elemental Powers 1.30B / 3,687; Youtuber Tycoon 819M / 2,305; Clone Tycoon 2 798M / 167; Ninja Tycoon 586M / 2,063; Ultra Power 515M / 308; Anime Tycoon 293M / 191. Huge reach and short sessions (10-13 min average per Rolimons), and most have decayed. Ninja Tycoon and Elemental Powers are the exceptions that are still updated.
  - *Building/creative management sims:* Restaurant Tycoon 2 1.65B / 2,604; Theme Park Tycoon 2 1.62B / 5,944; My Restaurant! 889M / 507; Tropical Resort Tycoon 587M / 205; Airport Tycoon! 465M / 95; Mall Tycoon 435M / 381; Restaurant Tycoon 404M / 15; Hospital Tycoon 379M / 117; Retail Tycoon 2 340M / 2,088; Retail Tycoon 218M / 39. The leaders in this group are the most durable titles on the platform. The 2020-21 wave of business sims (mall, airport, resort, hospital) peaked at 12k-60k and then collapsed.
  - *Manual-work / resource tycoons:* Lumber Tycoon 2 1.34B / 5,077 (still near its own peak in 2026); Oaklands 61M / 1,635; Ultimate Mining Tycoon 90.5M / 1,879.
  - *Combat/war tycoons:* War Tycoon 1.13B / 13,083; Military Tycoon 1.01B / 3,685; Warfare Tycoon 142M / 2,692 (since 2010); 2 Player Military Tycoon 74M / 15. This is the healthiest sub-genre by current CCU per title.
  - *Collection/lifestyle hybrids:* Car Dealership Tycoon 2.54B / 10,037; Mega Mansion Tycoon 1.37B / 1,318; Mansion Tycoon 662M / 6,273; Giga Mansion Tycoon 333M / 6,470. The 2023 mansion tycoons hold 6k+ CCU and the highest like ratios of the set (96-97%).
  - *Sandbox/automation (factory) tycoons:* Miner's Haven 200M / 138; Factory Simulator 76M / 216; Industrialist 72M / 4,447; Islands (sandbox survival hybrid) 2.36B / 868. These have the longest sessions (43-51 min) but small, niche audiences, except Islands at its 2020 peak of 105,800.
  - *Two-player/co-op:* Wizard Tycoon - 2 Player 702M / 466; 2 Player Super Hero Tycoon 93M / 149; 2 Player Military Tycoon 74M / 15; 2 Player Gun Factory Tycoon 60M / 0. All are past their prime, though the format is still being produced in 2025-26 (e.g. MSL Games' "2 Player Fast Food Tycoon", 36.1M visits) ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7957168819)).
  - *Idle/incremental and RNG hybrids:* My Droplets 59.7M / 15; Tycoon RNG 40.8M / 11 (peak 32,019). Low durability.
  - *"Simulator"-branded store/service sims (2018-2024):* Supermarket Simulator 291M / 1,867; Zach's Service Station 195M / 138; Gas Station Simulator 69M / 19.
- **Branded tycoons:** Barbie Dreamhouse Tycoon (Gamefam x Barbie) and TMNT: Battle Tycoon (Gamefam x TMNT) appear in the Roblox wiki's event and search listings ([The Hunt: First Edition](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hunt:_First_Edition)). I have no stats for them.

### Inferences
- **Best track record (durability-weighted):** (1) collection/vehicle hybrid (CDT); (2) creative management sims with real simulation systems (TPT2, RT2, Retail Tycoon 2); (3) manual-work sandbox (LT2); (4) combat/war tycoons (War Tycoon, Military Tycoon). Classic droppers have the best record for **reach** and the worst for **retention**. Fad hybrids such as RNG are the worst for durability.
- Sub-genre matters less than whether there is a **non-terminal goal**. Every durable title replaces the classic "buy the final statue and you're done" ending ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)) with open-ended creation (parks, restaurants, bases), collection (600+ cars), or recurring PvP and capture-point conflict.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative, platform-wide breakdown of tycoon sub-genre share. The grouping above is my own classification of a sample of about 60 titles found through Roblox search.
- I could not get stats for branded tycoons (Barbie Dreamhouse Tycoon, TMNT: Battle Tycoon, Undead Defense Tycoon) because they were not in my API sample.

## Q3. Which tycoons had exceptionally long lifespans, and what explains their longevity?

### Takeaway
The evergreen tier is Theme Park Tycoon 2 (place from 2012, 5,944 CCU in Oct 2026), Lumber Tycoon 2 (beta Aug 2015, 5,077 CCU, record peak in Sep 2026), Car Dealership Tycoon (2018, 10,037 CCU), Restaurant Tycoon 2 (2019, 2,604 CCU, still updated in 2026), and the war tycoons (War Tycoon since 2020 at 13,083; Warfare Tycoon since 2010 at 2,692). The common factors are open-ended creative or collection goals, simulation depth, social sharing of builds, sustained updates and events (including Roblox platform events), and sequels made by the same developer. Durability is not explained by mechanics alone, because 2020-21 imitators of the business-sim format collapsed.

### Cited Findings
- **Theme Park Tycoon 2:** place created 2 Jan 2012 ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=31970568)). It was still updated on 31 Aug 2026 and drew 5,944 CCU ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=31970568)), with a Rolimons peak of 57,962 on 13 Jul 2024 and 8,498 in the last 30 days ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/69184822)). Systems behind its longevity: guest-needs simulation and park rating ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Den_S/Theme_Park_Tycoon_2)), custom-track coasters, co-building rights, 6-park servers so players can visit neighbours, optional-only gamepasses, and frequent updates ([TPT2 wiki](https://tpt2.fandom.com/wiki/Theme_Park_Tycoon_2)).
- **Lumber Tycoon 2:** "consistently seen over two thousand players playing concurrently since its beta release in August of 2015". It reached 1B visits on 26 Mar 2023. Its Rolimons all-time peak, 66,726, came in Sep 2023, five years after launch ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/1554960397)). Content levers: real licensed brands (Zenvo, 2024), recurring Monster Jam Halloween events since 2022, seasons, challenges and a trading hub ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Foxzie/Car_Dealership_Tycoon)).
- **Restaurant Tycoon 2:** about 500k daily active users and 7M monthly uniques in June 2023, nearly four years after its Oct 2019 launch. It is playable on "average smartphones" and is social (players visit and work in others' restaurants) ([IOM Today](https://www.iomtoday.co.im/news/entertainment/game-attracts-more-than-a-billion-plays-619340)). Its update log has continuous entries from Aug 2022 to Mar 2026: seasonal events, a drive-thru build mode, VIP tables, "Wheelchair Customers" ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/restaurant-tycoon-2-information/356970)). Its Rolimons peak was 65,251 on 26 May 2024, 4.5 years after launch ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/3398014311)).
- **The Hunt: First Edition (15-30 Mar 2024)** brought together many evergreen tycoons in one platform event: My Restaurant!, Car Dealership Tycoon, Elemental Powers Tycoon, Lumber Tycoon 2, Mega Mansion Tycoon, Military Tycoon, Restaurant Tycoon 2, War Tycoon, Undead Defense Tycoon and Barbie Dreamhouse Tycoon ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hunt:_First_Edition)).
- **Military Tycoon's persistence:** progress does not reset when a player leaves, unlike other tycoons ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/InfinityInteractive/Military_Tycoon)). That removes the main churn trigger of classic server-bound tycoons.
- **Miner's Haven:** deep endgame (rebirth, Reborn/Evolved items, Sacrifice at Life 1,000, lore) ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Berezaa_Games%E2%84%A2/Miner%27s_Haven)) and a 51-minute average session ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/258258996)). Yet it holds only 138 CCU after being open-sourced (Sep 2019) and handed to community developers ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Berezaa_Games%E2%84%A2/Miner%27s_Haven); [API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=105689481)).
- **Faded examples and their visible causes:**
  - Islands was discontinued by its developer in Nov 2023 and fell from a 105,800 peak to 868 CCU ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Easy.gg/Islands); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/4872321990)).
  - 2PGFT has not been updated since Jun 2023 and sits at 0 CCU ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=77232555)).
  - The Tycoon Games' clones (Anime, Super Hero and Ninja Tycoon) all stopped updating in Dec 2024 ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=2146451001,1860435730,1829380513)).
  - Tycoon RNG collapsed from 32,019 to under 20 within about two years ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/17601705136)).
  - Mall Tycoon fell from 59,870 (Mar 2021) to 381 CCU; Airport Tycoon! from 20,950 (Aug 2020) to 95; Tropical Resort Tycoon from 25,779 (Feb 2021) to 205 ([Rolimons Mall](https://www.rolimons.com/game/5736409216), [Airport](https://www.rolimons.com/game/3440262694), [TRT](https://www.rolimons.com/game/5534174456)).
- **Generic fade pattern (secondhand):** a developer describes a game losing players with ad spend bringing them back "only for a day or two". A Feb 2025 tycoon-style game, Build A Civilization, fell from 500+ CCU to about 50 "with no advertising change". CCU and session length are described as key discovery signals ([PSU, from a search summary; not fetched](https://www.psu.com/news/the-cheap-trick-roblox-devs-use-to-keep-dead-games-alive/)).
- **Sequels by the original developer** are a common way to keep a franchise alive: Retail Tycoon (2015) to Retail Tycoon 2 (2020); Restaurant Tycoon (2017) to RT2 (2019) to RT3 (2025); Tropical Resort Tycoon (2020) to TRT2 (2023); Mega Mansion Tycoon (2021) to MMT2 (2025); Airport Tycoon! (2019) to Airport Tycoon 2 (2026) ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=111535124,2092410051,331220105,1214576306,7094518649,1934249057,4343544885,3191268194,8724785394,1225802146,10756847038)). Haggie125's non-sequel follow-up, Cruise Ship Tycoon (2018), reached only 38.4M visits ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=940371198)).

### Inferences
- **Longevity drivers, ranked by how consistently they show up among the survivors:**
  - (1) An open-ended expressive or collection goal with no "final statue".
  - (2) Simulation feedback loops that make building decisions matter: guest needs and park rating in TPT2; store rating factors in Retail Tycoon; product margins in Retail Tycoon 2; worker happiness in RT2.
  - (3) Social showcase: visiting other players' parks and restaurants, co-building, fan-group base reviews, YouTube creator ranks.
  - (4) Steady live ops: updates, seasonal events, licensed brands, platform events like The Hunt.
  - (5) Cross-device reach: RT2 on Xbox and smartphones; LT2 with controller and mobile support.
- **Platform events can produce record peaks even for decade-old games.** LT2's record came in Sep 2026, 11 years after its beta, during a Roblox-run event. Evergreen tycoons look like good partners for Roblox events, and events in turn are a durable source of re-engagement.
- Developer continuity matters. Games that were abandoned (2PGFT), discontinued (Islands), or handed off/open-sourced (Miner's Haven) declined regardless of depth.

### Gaps
- No primary statements from Den_S, Defaultio or Foxzie explain their own longevity strategies. Everything above is inferred from features and stats.
- I found no year-by-year CCU history to separate update-driven spikes from organic decline (Rolimons charts are rendered by JavaScript and were not parsed).
- Several games (TPT2, EPT and Tycoon RNG) share a Rolimons peak date of 13 Jul 2024. I could not find the platform event that explains it. "The Games" ran 1-11 Aug 2024, so that is not the cause ([Fandom: The Games](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Games)).

## Q4. How did the genre evolve: the free-model tycoon kit era, the decline of low-quality clones, and the move to deeper management sims and hybrids?

### Takeaway
The genre began in January 2008 with uberubert's uncopylocked "Bread Factory Tycoon", which set the button-purchase standard and was widely copied. It had a 2015 resurgence powered by berezaa's and Zed_Gaming/Zednov's free tycoon kits, and hit peak saturation in 2016-2017, when Roblox's front page and YouTubers (DanTDM, FGTeeV, ItsFunneh) pushed tycoons. Bait-and-switch practices and botting were widespread in that period. From 2016 onward the genre moved toward customizable management sims (Restaurant/Retail/Theme Park Tycoon 2), from 2020-21 toward war tycoons and collection hybrids, and in 2023-24 toward mansion, branded and RNG hybrids.

### Cited Findings
- **2008, origins:** uberubert's "Bread Factory Tycoon" (January 2008) drew 36,000+ visits. Because it was uncopylocked, "many scripters took advantage of this and started creating their own tycoons" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)). It "was the first to use the standard button system of purchasing upgrades and utilizing a money leader-board". Its mechanics included owner and repairman roles plus sabotage upgrades ([Fandom: Bread Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Uberubert/Bread_Tycoon)).
- **2008-2009, early sandbox and manual tycoons:** Dued1's Your Store Tycoon was the first sandbox tycoon. Dates conflict: "in 2009" per the genre page ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)), "March 2008" per its own infobox ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Dued1/Your_Store_Tycoon)). Defaultio's Lumber Tycoon (23 Oct 2008) inspired 2009 copies ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Defaultio/Lumber_Tycoon)).
- **2014-2015, the kit era:** Zed_Gaming (formerly "Zednov", originally "MarcsMinion") rose to fame in 2014 and is "more known as a famous Tycoon Developer". He published "Zednov's Tycoon Kit" (Roblox catalog item 316073214), "similar" to berezaa's kit ([Fandom: Zed_Gaming](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Zed_Gaming)).
  - The same wiki page claims the kit "is used by about 90% of tycoons now on ROBLOX" because it needs no scripting. That is an **unsourced wiki claim**, so treat it as anecdotal ([Fandom: Zed_Gaming](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Zed_Gaming)).
  - His listed games include Noob Defense Tycoon, Egg Factory Tycoon, Murder Tycoon, Advanced Murder Tycoon, Ninja Dojo Tycoon, ROBLOX Battle Tycoon, 2 Player SF Tycoon, Candy Warfare Tycoon, Space Wars Tycoon and Advanced Warfare Tycoon ([same](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Zed_Gaming)).
  - berezaa is "known for his Tycoon Creating Kit that has substantially helped users on Roblox become successful tycoon makers" ([Fandom: Berezaa](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Berezaa)). The genre page credits "around 2015" with a "significant resurgence, largely due to" berezaa and Zed_Gaming, whose kits "allowed many users to create their own tycoons with minimal effort" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)).
- **2016-2017, front-page peak and YouTuber amplification:** "By 2016-2017, as Roblox began promoting tycoon experiences more on the front page, YouTubers such as DanTDM, FGTeeV, and ItsFunneh played numerous tycoon experiences, with many of their videos receiving over 10 million views" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)). McDonald's Tycoon was on the front page in 2017, was later deleted, and was reopened as "McRonalds Tycoon" ([same](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)). Super Hero Tycoon (Dec 2016), Clone Tycoon 2 (May 2016), Pizza Factory Tycoon (Aug 2016) and Restaurant Tycoon (Apr 2017) date from this window ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=228573408,156428258,180358774,331220105)).
- **Clone, bait-and-switch and botting problems:**
  - Zed_Gaming "used the 'bait and switch' method to mislead others into his game. Along with many other developers at the time". He was terminated on 10 Aug 2016 after Roblox judged his Robux tycoon game a "scam game", and again on an alt in Jul 2017 ([Fandom: Zed_Gaming](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Zed_Gaming)).
  - Deceptive tactics of the era: false thumbnails (often using famous YouTubers), misleading titles, game-teleporters and like-bots. Roblox adjusted the Top Rated algorithm in April 2015 and now requires a play before rating. "From late 2017 to mid-2019, botting into the popular section, using many thousands of bot accounts, was common", but "since 2019, the problem of botting has been significantly reduced" ([Fandom: Deceptive advertising](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Deceptive_advertising)).
  - Super Hero Tycoon spawned "thousands" of near-identical thumbnails tied to deceptive ads ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Heroes%E2%84%A2/Super_Hero_Tycoon)).
- **Paid-access betas (2015-2017):** Retail Tycoon and Restaurant Tycoon both charged 25 Robux for beta access before going free ([Fandom Retail](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Haggie125/Retail_Tycoon); [Fandom Restaurant](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Ultraw/Restaurant_Tycoon)).
- **Recognition of deeper tycoons:**
  - 2015 BLOXY Awards: LT2 nominated for Favorite Breakout Game, Best Single Player, Most Hours Played and Game of the Year; Miner's Haven nominated for Favorite Breakout and Most Hours Played ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/2015_BLOXY_Awards)).
  - 4th Annual Bloxys: TPT2 won the Builderman Award of Excellence; TPT2, LT2 and Miner's Haven were nominated for Game of the Year ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/4th_Annual_Bloxy_Awards)).
  - berezaa won a Community Excellence Award for Miner's Haven (2016) ([Fandom: Berezaa](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Berezaa)).
  - 5th Bloxys: Restaurant Tycoon won Best Tutorial ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/5th_Annual_Bloxy_Awards)).
  - RIA 2024: TPT2 won Best Tycoon ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Roblox_Innovation_Awards_2024)).
- **Roblox Accelerator:** LT2's development started around 29 May 2015, when Defaultio joined Roblox's first Accelerator program ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Defaultio/Lumber_Tycoon_2); [LT2 wiki](https://lumber-tycoon-2.fandom.com/wiki/Lumber_Tycoon_2)). The Accelerator was a 12-week program in which developers worked with producers on an original game; it ended in autumn 2022 ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Accelerator_program)).
- **2016 onward, the shift to customizable sims:** "Starting in 2016, some modern tycoons have begun to diverge from the old model, by becoming more like simulators designed around customizability" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)). Ultraw's own catalogue shows the path: conveyor tycoons (Clone Tycoon 2, 2016), then a conveyor tycoon with AI customers (Pizza Factory Tycoon, 2016) ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Ultraw/Pizza_Factory_Tycoon)), then the full management sims Restaurant Tycoon (2017) and RT2 (2019) ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Ultraw/Restaurant_Tycoon)).
- **2019, open-sourcing:** berezaa released Miner's Haven's source under Apache 2 on 16 Sep 2019 ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Berezaa_Games%E2%84%A2/Miner%27s_Haven)).
- **2020-2022, management-sim gold rush, war tycoons and IP friction:**
  - Management sims: Mall Tycoon, Tropical Resort Tycoon, Retail Tycoon 2, Hospital Tycoon and Airport Tycoon! peaked between Aug 2020 and Jan 2022 ([Rolimons, Q1 table](https://www.rolimons.com/game/5736409216)).
  - War tycoons: War Tycoon (2020) and Military Tycoon (2021) took over the combat niche ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)).
  - IP friction: the Skyblock-derived Islands was DMCA-deleted in Jul 2020 and relaunched under a new name ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Easy.gg/Islands)).
- **2023-2024, mansion/home tycoons, branded tycoons and RNG hybrids:**
  - Mansion/home: Mansion Tycoon (Mar 2023), Giga Mansion Tycoon (Jul 2023) and Ultimate Home Tycoon (Nov 2023) ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=4518160318,4925331346,5295169598)).
  - Branded: Barbie Dreamhouse Tycoon and TMNT: Battle Tycoon (both Gamefam) ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hunt:_First_Edition)).
  - RNG hybrid: Tycoon RNG (May 2024), a spike and collapse ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/17601705136)).
  - Store sims under the "Simulator" name: Supermarket Simulator (Oct 2024) ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=6657933195)).
- **Genre roots outside Roblox (brief):** TPT2's influences are Bullfrog's *Theme Park* (1994) and Chris Sawyer's *RollerCoaster Tycoon* (1999). This is a search summary of a VidaExtra article, not fetched ([VidaExtra](https://www.vidaextra.com/casual-games/roblox-no-sera-portento-visual-que-se-pueda-crear-theme-park-que-me-parece-impresionante-multijugador-online)). The Roblox wiki compares TPT2 to RollerCoaster Tycoon and Planet Coaster ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Den_S/Theme_Park_Tycoon_2)).

### Inferences
- **Free kits drove volume and pulled quality down.** Kits made the classic loop almost free to produce, which flooded discovery with near-identical tycoons during the 2016-2017 front-page window and invited deceptive thumbnails and botting. Only a few themed classic droppers that kept updating (Super Hero Tycoon, Ninja Tycoon, later Elemental Powers Tycoon) kept meaningful audiences. Whether these were built on kits is not verified.
- **Quality rose, but cheap clones never went away.** Clone groups (The Tycoon Games, the "Tycoon Empire" groups) still produced 50-300M-visit games in 2019-2021, then abandoned them. Durable success moved to games with real systems (simulation, PvP, collection) and live operations.
- The genre went through four phases. Visits are spread across all of them, but **current** CCU is concentrated in phase (b) and later:
  - (a) idle droppers, 2008-2017;
  - (b) creative management sims, 2016-2020;
  - (c) PvP/war and collection hybrids, 2018-2022;
  - (d) lifestyle, branded and "RNG/steal" hybrids, 2023 onward.

### Gaps
- The berezaa tycoon kit's catalog ID, release date and download count were not found. Zednov's kit catalog page was not fetched, so its creation date is unverified.
- I found no quantitative source on how many tycoons were removed for bait-and-switch, or on when Roblox stopped promoting tycoons on the front page.
- I have no figure for how many tycoon games exist on Roblox, or how many have more than 1M visits.

## Q5. What did standout developers say in interviews, DevForum posts or talks about building these games?

### Takeaway
On-record developer commentary is scarce. The best primary material is Haggie125's 2018 interview on Retail Tycoon and Ultraw creator Cameron Scott's 2023 interview on Restaurant Tycoon 2. berezaa contributed public statements and the open-sourcing of Miner's Haven. Recurring themes: surprise at organic growth, iterating on a first version (sequels built "upon what I had already"), NPC and AI simulation as the fun part, rewrites caused by lack of planning, and continuous content updates. I found no interviews for Den_S (TPT2), Defaultio (LT2), Foxzie (CDT), KizmoTek (War Tycoon), InfinityInteractive (Military Tycoon) or Wild Atelier (MMT).

### Cited Findings
- **Haggie125 (Retail Tycoon), interview of 17 Sep 2018** ([Roblox Wiki developer interview](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:RBLXDeveloperInterviews/Haggie125:_Retail_Tycoon)):
  - He was surprised the game "exploded" with thousands of players online during beta, before he had advertised, and said that added pressure.
  - Poor planning and inexperience meant he rewrote most of the game, but he said he learned a great deal about building one.
  - His favourite part was writing NPC logic: "watch a hundred little artificial people run around doing their own thing".
  - Being featured by Roblox was "not too much work" and gave Retail Tycoon its best month on record.
  - He was putting his "newfound experience" into Cruise Ship Tycoon. That game later reached only 38.4M visits ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=940371198)). His studio's direct sequel, Retail Tycoon 2, reached 340M ([API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=2092410051)).
- **Cameron Scott, creator of Restaurant Tycoon 2 (Ultraw), IOM Today, 8 Jun 2023** ([IOM Today](https://www.iomtoday.co.im/news/entertainment/game-attracts-more-than-a-billion-plays-619340)):
  - He started coding around age 14.
  - The restaurant idea came from a friend's suggestion in 2017.
  - The first version succeeded, so in 2019, with more programming experience, he built a second version that let him "build upon what I had already".
  - RT2 took about 6 months to build and gained popularity "almost instantly" after its October 2019 release.
  - Updates are continuous (café food, dishwashers, furniture). He stresses that the game runs on average smartphones, suits all ages, and is social and educational.
  - Naming conflict: the fetched article calls him "Cameron Scott" ("Mr Scott"). A search-engine snippet of the same article said "Cameron Triggs". The fetched page text is used here.
- **Ultraw Games' studio info:** the studio is based in the Isle of Man and credits outside modelers (e.g. Zynkuru) and community translators ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/restaurant-tycoon-2-information/356970)). Xbox Wire credits "Ultraw and Zynkuru" as creators and pitches RT2 as "a management sim that's just as much about indulging your creative side" ([Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2020/08/13/feed-your-creativity-in-restaurant-tycoon-2-on-roblox/)). That framing is Xbox's, not a direct developer quote.
- **berezaa (Andrew Bereza):**
  - He called 2PGFT "an unexpected success" that reached the front page in 2014 ([Miner's Haven wiki](https://minershaven.fandom.com/wiki/Berezaa)).
  - He open-sourced Miner's Haven and Azure Mines ([Fandom: Berezaa](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Berezaa)).
  - In a 23 Jan 2019 tweet he criticized the Roblox Video Stars program for how it "selectively and artificially bolsters certain creators", relevant because YouTubers drove the 2016-17 tycoon boom ([Fandom: Berezaa](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Berezaa)).
  - He was interviewed by Business Insider (article not located) and did a Reddit AMA with Roblox's VP of Developer Relations in Nov 2018 ([Fandom: Berezaa](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Berezaa)).
  - A New Jersey Monthly profile (search summary only, not fetched) quotes Roblox CEO David Baszucki calling him one of the platform's top developers. It reports that many creators then earned $500-1,000 a month and some more than $20,000 ([NJ Monthly](https://njmonthly.com/articles/jersey-living/teenage-gamer-turns-a-profit/)).
- **Defaultio (LT2):** the only documented process fact is that LT2's development began in Roblox's first Accelerator program in May 2015 ([Fandom](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Defaultio/Lumber_Tycoon_2)). No interview was found (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- Across the two detailed interviews, success comes from **iterating on a proven concept** (Retail Tycoon to RT2; Restaurant Tycoon to RT2) more than from new IP. Haggie125's new-IP follow-up (Cruise Ship Tycoon) underperformed sharply, while both studios' numbered sequels did about 1.5-4x better than their originals (Retail Tycoon 2: 340M vs 218M; Restaurant Tycoon 2: 1.65B vs 404M).
- Developers of the durable sims describe **NPC/AI behaviour and player creativity** as what makes the game. That matches the simulation and creative-expression longevity drivers in Q3.

### Gaps
- I found no primary interviews, DevForum postmortems or RDC talks from Den_S, Defaultio, Foxzie, KizmoTek Studio, InfinityInteractive, Wild Atelier, Giggle Inc or Coldjason. Searches returned only fan wikis and SEO pages.
- berezaa's Medium posts, including the Miner's Haven open-source post, returned HTTP 403 ([Medium](https://medium.com/@berezaa)). The Business Insider interview could not be located.
- No developer-disclosed revenue, DevEx or retention figures were found for any classic tycoon.
