# Roblox tycoon and tycoon-hybrid market, 2025-2026: what is on top, what is trending, and how discovery works now

Research date: 18 October 2026. Every figure is dated where the source allows. Player counts (CCU) come mostly from third-party trackers (RoWatcher, GGAID, Rolimon's, rblxdb, RoVitals, Profitable.app). Roblox does not publish per-game CCU, and trackers often disagree. Revenue figures for individual games are modelled estimates and are flagged as such. Official Roblox numbers come from shareholder letters (SEC 8-K exhibits), the Roblox newsroom, Creator Hub docs and DevForum announcements.

---

## 1. Which tycoon and tycoon-hybrid games are on top now, and which broke out in 2025-2026?

### Takeaway
The biggest Roblox hits of 2025-2026 are nearly all idle or tycoon hybrids: passive income, collecting, and (more and more) stealing from other players. Grow a Garden (peak ~22M CCU, Aug 2025), Steal a Brainrot (peak ~25.4-25.8M CCU, Oct 2025) and now **Steal An Egg** (launched July 2026, peak ~14.2-14.3M CCU on 19 Sept 2026, still ~1.2-1.9M CCU in late Sept/early Oct 2026) are the defining examples. Most 2025 viral hits have since dropped 90-99% from their peaks. Classic named "Tycoon" titles are not in the top charts in 2026.

### Inferences
- In late 2026 the commercial centre of Roblox is **idle/tycoon cores with social conflict (stealing) plus collection/RNG (eggs, pets, mutations, rarities)**. Steal An Egg is in effect "Steal a Brainrot's loop plus Pet Simulator's hatch/mutation collection". The top earners of the last 18 months are tycoon-shaped even when they aren't called "Tycoon".
- Viral peaks are huge and short. Grow a Garden 2 went from ~976K to under 21K in about three and a half months, and Animal Hospital lost 90% in about two months. Sustained CCU in the 100-300K range (Steal a Brainrot, 99 Nights, Fish It!) is a more realistic "success" target than a 10M+ spike.
- The word "Tycoon" in a title is not what the top charts reward. Simple verb-noun titles ("Steal An Egg", "Sell Lemons", "Grow a Garden", "Kick a Lucky Block") dominate.

### Gaps
- No official per-game CCU or revenue. All revenue numbers are third-party models with wide disagreement.
- Unverified: 99 Nights all-time peak, Plants vs Brainrots peak, Escape Tsunami peak (sources conflict).
- Developer and studio identity of Steal An Egg and Grow a Garden 2 is not clearly established in reliable sources.
- No 2026 CCU found for War Tycoon, Military Tycoon, Restaurant Tycoon 3 or Theme Park Tycoon 2. I also didn't find a reliable 2026 source on "Build a Zoo", "Build an Island" or Dig-type mining tycoons, so their status is unknown.
- An early-2026 article claimed "Steal An Egg ~3 million active players", which conflicts with other sources and was not used.

## 2. What made the 2025-2026 breakout hits work, and which mechanics do they share?

### Takeaway
The hits share a small set of mechanics:
- Instantly readable verb-noun fantasy
- Passive or offline income
- Rarity-tiered collection (seeds, brainrots, eggs and pets with mutations)
- Social risk: stealing or raiding with base defenses
- Rotating limited stock
- Weekly updates with scheduled "admin abuse" or crossover events and celebrity tie-ins
- Very low skill floor

The hits went viral on TikTok and YouTube. Most were also criticised for pay-to-win monetization.

### Cited Findings
- **Grow a Garden loop:** plant and harvest crops for Sheckles, buy rarer seeds, and crops keep growing offline. Seed stock rotates and exclusive items appear weekly to pull players back. Pets come from loot boxes, and Robux buys growth speed, perks, or stealing crops. Named weekly updates included Blood Moon (18 May), Bizzy Bees (31 May), Working Bees (14 June) and the Summer Update (21 June), plus a Travis Kelce appearance and the 23 Aug "admin war". Critics called it a "pretty standard AFK game". — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- **Jandel's explanation:** a farming theme that is "as generic as you can get"; the persistent world where "You return and your plants have grown bigger", which he called novel for Roblox; and Splitting Point's weekly live update cadence. — [GamesBeat, Sept 2025](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)
- **Steal a Brainrot loop:** start with a base; buy Brainrots off a conveyor belt; each Brainrot earns passive income. Buy rarer ones or steal them from other bases (compared to capture the flag). A base button raises a temporary shield. Traps and items cost in-game currency or Robux. Rebirth resets progress for better stats. Developer-run "admin abuse" events hand out exclusive characters. It went viral on TikTok and YouTube, including clips of children upset about losing their Brainrots. Polygon criticised the pay-to-win design (best items cash-only, admin abilities for sale). It won Best Creative Direction at the 2025 Roblox Innovation Awards. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- **Licensing and IP:** Steal a Brainrot was licensed to Fortnite Creative as "Steal the Brainrot" (July 2025, peak ~400-500K CCU). The developer sued an unlicensed Fortnite copy (Oct 2025), and a film adaptation is in development. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot). The developer has reportedly filed at least four lawsuits against imitators since Nov 2025. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-brainrot-dev-sues-fortnite-creator-over-alleged-clone) (via search snippet)
- **Cross-game events drive record peaks:** the 23 Aug 2025 "admin war" between Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot pushed both games to records and the platform to its concurrency record. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot). Steal An Egg's 14.27M peak (19 Sept 2026) came from a crossover with Steal a Brainrot's creator. — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-september-2026/). Steal a Brainrot's Jan 2026 Bruno Mars concert hit 12.8M CCU. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)
- **Steal An Egg loop:** steal eggs from pets and players, hatch them for rare pets that earn money, upgrade base and treadmill, and train Speed to reach rarer eggs, sizes and mutations. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10563114921/steal-an-egg). Speed could be bought with Robux or ground out on the treadmill. — [eGamers](https://egamers.io/roblox-bans-games-that-pay-kids-to-keep-scrolling-in-wake-of-steal-an-egg-uproar/). Player.One credits its "simple, easily understood objective" plus PvP stealing that creates social stories. — [Player.One](https://www.player.one/robloxs-steal-egg-beating-some-platforms-biggest-games-164086). Weekly updates drove its surge. — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-september-2026/)
- **The steal mechanic spreading:** Grow a Garden 2 (June 2026) bolted night-time garden raiding, defensive crops and guilds onto the farming loop. — [GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/78614-grow-a-garden-2-has-launched-on-roblox-here-s-everything-new-added-in-this-sequel)
- **What sustains the genre:** collectibles, tycoon loops, regular events, celebrity collaborations and relentless weekly content drops. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- **Discovery tailwind:** after the Recommended For You (RFY) algorithm went global in March 2025, the #1 game routinely passed 7B monthly visits, against a pre-RFY ceiling of 2.2B. Grow a Garden took 48.1% of Top-20 visits in June 2025, and Steal a Brainrot held 35-40% for six straight months (Aug-Dec 2025). — [Dubit, 7 Feb 2026](https://dubit.io/blog/roblox-top-20-rfy)
- **Speed of take-off:** Grow a Garden went from ~500 CCU to ~100K in two weeks and ~1M in about a month. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/janzen-madsen-interview/)

### Inferences
- Shared formula: (1) a one-line fantasy in the title; (2) an idle or passive income core that also pays offline; (3) rarity-tiered collectibles, now usually with mutations or sizes; (4) loss aversion from stealing or raiding, offset by locks, shields, traps or defensive items; (5) time-gated scarcity (rotating stock, weekly exclusives); (6) weekly updates; (7) scheduled "admin abuse" or live events and cross-game crossovers that concentrate CCU into spikes; (8) TikTok-friendly emotional moments (getting robbed).
- Crossovers between top creators (Sammy, Jandel and others) are now a repeatable tactic for record peaks. A newcomer can't access that network directly, but in-game live events and "admin abuse" sessions are copyable.
- The stealing loop also raises intentional co-play and return visits. That fits the RFY signals in section 4, which may be part of why it was so heavily rewarded.
- Pay-to-win criticism and kid-safety scrutiny now follow these designs (see sections 3 and 5). Monetizing stealing advantages directly carries reputational and policy risk.

### Gaps
- No primary design postmortem from Steal a Brainrot's or Steal An Egg's developers was found.
- No verified retention or D1/D7 figures for any of these hits were found.
- No reliable source found on the trading economy in these games (Roblox Plus added Robux trading and transfer features; see section 5).

## 3. Trending vs fading themes in 2026: is the steal/brainrot wave saturated?

### Takeaway
The "Italian brainrot" meme is fading. Its flagship has lost about 99% of peak CCU, and brainrot spinoffs peak lower each time. The **"steal" mechanic itself is not dead**: it carried Steal An Egg to a 14M peak in Sept 2026 by being reskinned onto pets and eggs. The rising mix in late 2026 is steal/egg/pet hybrids, anime idle/RNG, and short-session horror sims. Roblox is now actively pushing discovery and payouts toward retentive, "novel" and older-audience games, which works against quick viral copycats.

### Cited Findings
- **Brainrot normalising:** RoWatcher (around late March 2026) argues the trend is "normalizing" into an established genre rather than dying. It says the explosive phase is over and no brainrot game is likely to reach 25M again without a rare event. It also notes Italian Brainrot was declared "dead" online in Dec 2025, and that each Steal a Brainrot peak was followed by a sharper drop. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- **Diminishing brainrot spinoff peaks:** Escape Tsunami for Brainrots ~5M (Jan 2026) and Swing Obby for Brainrots ~87K (Feb-Mar 2026). — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)
- **Sept 2026 movers:** "Steal / egg / pet" loops dominate the board. Steal An Egg was up 468% and Ride A Pet was new in the top 10. "+1 Speed" games are fading fast: +1 Speed Keyboard Escape fell 61.5%, and +1 Speed Monkey Escape dropped out of the top 50. Anime is rising: Slayers 2 launched 18 Sept 2026 at 115-186K CCU, and Anime Dice (idle + dice RNG, weekly updates) reached 75.8K. — [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-september-2026/); [EndGameTools](https://endgametools.com/en/roblox/news/roblox-most-popular-games)
- **Roblox's own view (Q2 2026 letter, 30 July 2026):** engagement "shifted more than expected from high-monetizing 2025-vintage viral games to new and evergreen games with lower hourly monetization". Per-hour monetization declined "most notably with younger cohorts in the U.S. and Canada". — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)
- Roblox said as early as Q3 2025 that "viral hits from the summer have started to decline while new hits are emerging", and that timing and longevity of hits are hard to predict. — [Q3 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)
- **Policy against "scroll-to-earn":** in late Aug 2026, after Steal An Egg, Roblox told developers that "games that reward ongoing media feed consumption" are no longer permitted in Roblox Kids and Select. That covers games combining media feeds, autoplay, infinite scroll or autoscroll with rewards or implied rewards for watching. — [eGamers, 27 Aug 2026](https://egamers.io/roblox-bans-games-that-pay-kids-to-keep-scrolling-in-wake-of-steal-an-egg-uproar/); MassivelyOP headline: "Roblox clamps down on gen AI brainrot scrolling games after Steal an Egg goes viral" — [MassivelyOP, 29 Aug 2026](https://massivelyop.com/2026/08/29/roblox-clamps-down-on-gen-ai-brainrot-scrolling-games-after-steal-an-egg-goes-viral/)
- **Shift toward older and "novel" games:** from 8 June 2026, Roblox raised the DevEx rate by 42% for spend from age-checked U.S. 18+ users in eligible "novel" games that use R15 avatars, lifting the effective creator share of that spend from 26.6% to 37.8%. Roblox defines novel games as ones that "expand into new genres, use different game play mechanics, and have a different look and feel than classic Roblox games". The U.S. 18-34 cohort grew 50%+ YoY and monetizes 50% higher than under-18s. — [Roblox newsroom, Apr 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42); [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm)
- **Age-aware ranking (RDC 2026, Sept 2026):** ranking will recommend shorter games to younger players and deeper, return-visit games to older players. — [PocketGamer.biz, 14 Sept 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)
- **Copycat flood visible on trackers:** RoWatcher lists numerous derivative titles such as "Steal a Egg", "Break and Steal an Egg", "Jump To Steal An Egg", "STEAL a Brainrot or DIE", "Steal A Brainrot Egg", "Grow Brainrot Garden", "99 Nights Store Tycoon" and "Don't Steal the 99 Nights In The Forest". — [RoWatcher search results, e.g.](https://rowatcher.com/games/10765288803/break-and-steal-an-egg), [99 Nights Store Tycoon](https://rowatcher.com/games/9636446684/99-nights-store-tycoon). Clones of the "Don't Wake the..." trend sat at 31, 11, 7 and 2 CCU while the original "Don't Steal the Bobo" had ~9.5K. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/why-nobody-finds-your-roblox-game-and-the-blue-ocean-strategy-that-fixes-it)

### Inferences
- **Saturated:** brainrot IP and meme theming; literal "Steal a [noun]" titles; "+1 Speed" escape obbies. **Still working:** the steal/raid social layer as one feature inside a richer collection or tycoon loop (Steal An Egg, Grow a Garden 2).
- The steal hook may get a short-term boost from Steal An Egg's success, but any new "Steal a X" will compete against Steal An Egg's 1M+ CCU, Steal a Brainrot's 100-200K and thousands of clones. Roblox's docs also say look-alike metadata and place files are deprioritised (section 4).
- Policy direction: Roblox is under child-safety scrutiny. eGamers reports a U.S. Senate investigation opened about two weeks before 27 Aug 2026 and several state probes. Mechanics that look exploitative toward kids (scroll-for-reward, aggressive pay-to-steal) carry rising moderation and visibility risk.
- An opportunity worth considering: tycoons with deeper progression and multi-week retention, co-op base building, or themes that appeal to 13+ and 18+ players. Discovery and DevEx incentives now favour these over short viral loops.

### Gaps
- No quantitative measure found of how many "steal"-type or brainrot games launch per month, or what share succeed.
- No dated, reliable data found on emerging tycoon themes such as zoo, island, mining, restaurant or retail in 2026.
- The full Roblox DevForum notice text for the media-feed policy wasn't fetched. Its exact scope beyond Kids and Select is unconfirmed.

## 4. How does Roblox discovery work now, and which metrics does it reward?

### Takeaway
The Home "Recommended For You" (RFY) sort, global since March 2025 and the main traffic source, ranks games on **per-user engagement quality from organically acquired players**. Since **15 June 2026** it measures retention over **Day 1, Day 2-7 and Day 8-28** windows. The click-and-entry signals are play-through rate (PTR) and first-play bounce rate; the old qualified play-through rate (qPTR) is gone. The engagement signals are play days, playtime (capped at 60 min per user per day), intentional co-play days, qualified sessions, spend days and Robux spent. Roblox says outright that it now trades near-term monetization for retention. Look-alike games are deprioritised, and ad-acquired users don't count toward ranking.

### Cited Findings

**Timeline**
- **Mar 2025:** RFY activated globally, ranking on engagement quality (qPTR, weekly playtime, intentional co-play) rather than raw popularity. — [Dubit, 7 Feb 2026](https://dubit.io/blog/roblox-top-20-rfy)
- **Q3 2025:** signals included qPTR, 7-day play days per user and 7-day intentional co-play days. Creators got a benchmark dashboard. Unique experiences surfaced per 1M recommendations rose 20% YoY. Content ratings became mandatory and millions of unrated experiences were removed. Moments (short video feed) entered beta, and over 30% of viewers join games from the feed. — [Q3 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)
- **Dec 2025:** incremental signal adjustments. — [Dubit](https://dubit.io/blog/roblox-top-20-rfy)
- **Q1 2026 (letter dated 30 Apr 2026):** testing algorithms and homepage layouts optimised for 28-day retention, expected to weigh on Q2 engagement and bookings. "Standout Games" get priority on the second row of Home. Personalised home screens by user age are planned. — [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm)
- **15 June 2026:** RFY expanded from a 7-day to a 28-day view. Signals measured over D1, D2-7 and D8-28: playtime, play days, qualified play sessions, intentional co-play days, spend days and Robux spent. "We're removing QPTR and introducing PTR and first play bounce rates." Each signal counts only users who joined organically from the RFY sort. Benchmark games don't affect ranking. Data is in Creator Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations. Thumbnail personalization will be tested against PTR. — [DevForum announcement, 15 June 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)
- **Roblox's stated aim:** short-term engagement was favouring "games that win attention with exciting thumbnails but don't deliver long-term value". "Creators who build games that create long-term player value through retention, co-play, and spend will have more opportunity to build a lasting audience." — [Roblox newsroom, 15 June 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)
- **Business impact:** the RFY change "intentionally provides more impressions for highly retentive games at the expense of near term monetization". The near-term impact on younger cohorts was larger than expected. — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm). Q3 2026 bookings guidance is $1,576-1,653M (-14% to -18% YoY), and full-year guidance was withdrawn. — [same](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm). Shares fell nearly 14% after hours. — [Outlook Respawn](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-q2-2026-revenue-soars-36-to-15b-despite-bookings-miss) (snippet)
- **RDC 2026 (Sept 2026):** ranking becoming age-aware (shorter games for younger players, deeper return-visit games for older). Roblox is testing a "direct growth" signal that counts how many brand-new players a game brings to the platform. Moments is fully available in the U.S., and items shown in clips are purchasable from October. — [PocketGamer.biz, 14 Sept 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)

**Current signal definitions (Creator Hub "Discovery" doc, fetched Oct 2026)**
- **Most important:** PTR (rate of play after seeing the game in RFY); first-play bounce rate (negative; segments under 60s and 61-180s); play days per user (D1, D2-7, D8-28); playtime per user (capped at 60 min per user, per game, per day; same windows).
- **Important:** intentional co-play days per user (returning with friends via join, invites or private servers); qualified play sessions per user; spend days per user; Robux spent per user.
- Signals are per-user averages "so smaller games are not disadvantaged". "Games that have even a small number of people playing can signal to the system that the game is worth considering." — [Roblox Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- **Originality rules:** "Games with metadata and place files that closely resemble existing games on Roblox" are "no longer prioritized for recommendations and might rank lower in search results". Creators are told to "Avoid publishing content with repetitive titles and images that have been previously published". Metadata that mismatches gameplay is less visible. "Metadata that implies any type of monetary reward is not prioritized". The dashboard shows a banner when exposure is reduced. — [Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- **Ads:** Sponsored placements (via Ads Manager) target audience segments, but ranking "doesn't count the engagement, monetization, or retention of users first acquired from ads". Sponsored signals can still accelerate organic consideration. — [Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- **Search** is semantic and natural-language, e.g. "food games". **Standout Games** are hand-curated for novel mechanics, distinctive visuals or underrepresented genres, with no formal application process described. — [Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)

**Effect on chart turnover**
- After RFY, brand-new games (under 6 months of data) went from 1.3% to 60.7% of Top-20 visits. Mature games fell from 84% to 18% of Top-20 share, though their absolute visits held at 4.0-5.9B per month. Median Top-20 tenure fell from 14 months (July 2024) to 7 months (Dec 2025), and only 7 of the July 2024 Top 20 remained by Dec 2025. — [Dubit, 7 Feb 2026](https://dubit.io/blog/roblox-top-20-rfy)
- Top 10 games were ~20% of hours in Q2 2026, down from ~30% three years earlier. Experiences outside the top 10 grew hours 25% and Robux spend 20%+ YoY. — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm). In Q3 2025, new experiences (created within 12 months) in the Top 100 by engagement were up 50%+ vs Q3 2024. — [Q3 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)

**Ads and Ads Manager**
- About 18,000 creators used traffic-driving ads in Q3 2025, up 27% QoQ, and cost per play fell 30%+ vs Q2 2025. — [Q3 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)
- New 2x1 sponsored tiles launched, and more than 60 of the top 100 spend-based creators use native Ads Manager. — [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm)
- Ads Manager has a beta "Engagement" objective that targets Highly Engaged Players, aimed at helping games reach Kids and Select eligibility (section 5). Costs may exceed normal cost per play, and Roblox suggests running 3-5 days before judging. — [DevForum, 16 June 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-global-launch-upcoming-updates-to-eligibility-ads-manager-and-expedited-review/4685717)

**Community benchmarks (unofficial)**
- Community analysis (BLOXG, via RoWatcher) cites D1 retention above ~20%, D7 above ~8% and sessions of 15+ minutes as strong. These are not Roblox figures. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/why-nobody-finds-your-roblox-game-and-the-blue-ocean-strategy-that-fixes-it)
- DevForum commenters on the June 2026 change asked how brand-new games get evaluated when signals take up to 28 days. The post did not describe a cold-start mechanism. — [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)

### Inferences
- **What to optimise for a new tycoon (in rough order of weight):**
  1. Thumbnail and title PTR.
  2. Making the first 60-180 seconds sticky to cut bounce: instant income, a clear goal, first collectible within a minute.
  3. Multi-day return reasons over D2-7 and D8-28: offline earnings, rotating stock, daily and weekly events.
  4. Intentional co-play: friend-joined bases, shared plots, raids with friends, private servers.
  5. Spend *days*, i.e. frequent small purchases, not only whales.
- Playtime is capped at 60 min per user per day, so marathon AFK sessions add nothing beyond an hour. Return days matter more than long sessions. This penalises pure AFK-idle designs that rely on players leaving the game open.
- Paid ads can bootstrap a cold start and Kids/Select eligibility but don't directly lift ranking signals. Organic RFY users have to retain.
- A brand-new game is judged on organic RFY users over up to 28 days. Expect a slower, noisier ramp than the 2025 viral cycles, and plan for consistent updates during the first month.
- Originality now matters for discovery. A reskin of Steal a Brainrot or Steal An Egg with similar title, thumbnail or place file risks being deprioritised.

### Gaps
- Roblox shared the relative signal weights only as a chart image in the June 2026 DevForum post. Numeric weights couldn't be read.
- Charts page sort definitions (e.g. "Top Trending", "Up-and-Coming", "Top Earning", "Top Rated") aren't documented on the current Discovery page. I couldn't verify how they are computed in 2026. From background knowledge, not verified this session: Roblox replaced "Discover" with a "Charts" page around 2024.
- How the "direct growth" signal will be weighted, and when, is not public. It was described as a test in Sept 2026.
- No official cold-start policy for new games was found.

## 5. Roblox platform changes in 2025-2026 that matter for a new tycoon

### Takeaway
Six changes matter most:
1. **Mandatory facial age checks for chat** (global from Jan 2026)
2. **Age-tiered accounts** (Roblox Kids ages 5-8 and Select ages 9-15, global 16 June 2026). New games start visible only to age-checked 16+ users until they pass an engagement evaluation.
3. **Monetization additions:** regional pricing for all item types, rewarded video ads, Roblox Plus subscription
4. **DevEx raised to $3.80 per 1,000 Robux** (Sept 2025), plus a 42% DevEx bonus for U.S. 18+ spend in novel R15 games (June 2026)
5. **Cross-experience pass sales disabled** (May 2026)
6. **A ban on reward-for-scrolling** in Kids and Select (Aug 2026)

The macro backdrop is weaker monetization in younger cohorts and falling bookings guidance for Q3 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Platform scale and health**
- Q3 2025: 151.5M average DAU (+70% YoY), 39.6B hours (+91%), 35.8M monthly unique payers. — [Q3 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)
- Q1 2026: 132M DAU (+35% YoY), 31B hours, $1.73B bookings (+43%), 31M monthly unique payers. Growth slowed partly because of age checks (less chat, slower new-user acquisition) and the Dec 2025 Russia ban. — [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm)
- Q2 2026: 123M DAU (+10% YoY), 29B hours (+5%), $1,557M bookings (+8%, low end of guidance), 27M monthly unique payers. Q3 2026 bookings are guided down 14-18% YoY. — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)
- DAU grew 67% in Japan and 64% in India in Q2 2026, versus 6% in U.S./Canada. — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)

**Age checks and age-tiered accounts**
- Age checks were required to chat in Australia, New Zealand and the Netherlands from early Dec 2025 and rolled out globally in Jan 2026. Chat is grouped by age, and under-9s need parental consent. — [Roblox newsroom, Jan 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/01/roblox-age-checks-required-to-chat); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-rolls-out-mandatory-facial-age-checks-for-in-game-chat-worldwide)
- Morgan Stanley's early test-market data showed "no discernible impact" on engagement. That was preliminary. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/roblox-age-verification-rollout-shows-152656840.html). Roblox's Q1 2026 letter, however, says age checks restricted communication and slowed new-user acquisition. — [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm)
- Age-check penetration: 51% of global DAU in Q1 2026 (65% U.S.); 57% in Q2 2026 (U.S. and Australia above 70%, long-term goal 90%). Age-checked DAU split: 35% under 13, 38% aged 13-17, 27% 18+. On-platform communication is still below pre-age-check levels. — [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm); [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)
- **Roblox Kids (5-8), Roblox Select (9-15), Roblox (age-checked 16+).** Content allowed: Kids, Minimal or Mild; Select, up to Moderate; 16+, everything except Restricted (18+ only).
- **What a game needs to reach Kids and Select:** a creator account that is age-checked and verified, with 2-Step Verification; a one-time refundable fee or two consecutive months of Plus or Premium; and an evaluation. **New games are first available only to age-checked users 16+** until they reach **250 unique plays from highly engaged, age-checked players within 60 days**, along with engagement and safety review. Expedited review costs a refundable 50,000 Robux. — [Creator Docs: Kids and Select](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select)
- **Earlier version of these rules:** the 16 June 2026 DevForum post set the bar at 500 Highly Engaged Players with a 1,000 Robux refundable fee and expedited review at 100,000 Robux. The retention threshold was lowered from 100 to 25 HEP over 60 days (16 July update), and the HEP definition was relaxed (6 July), adding an estimated 33% more games to the catalog. — [DevForum, 16 June 2026](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-global-launch-upcoming-updates-to-eligibility-ads-manager-and-expedited-review/4685717). The current docs show the lower, later numbers.
- About 30,000 games were available to Kids and Select accounts by Q2 2026, up nearly 50% since launch. — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)
- IARC ratings are being adopted, and ratings became mandatory for all experiences in 2025. — [Q3 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)

**Monetization features**
- **Regional pricing:** launched Apr 2025, extending Price Optimization. Early tests raised the share of users spending by ~17% in Mexico, ~26% in Brazil and ~52% in the Philippines. — [Roblox newsroom, Apr 2025](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-launches-regional-pricing-for-in-experience-items). - **Roblox Plus subscription:** $4.99/month with item discounts, free private servers and Robux transfer and trading. Roblox funds the discount, and creators earn a higher effective share on Plus transactions. — [Q1 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026028882/ex991-q12026earningsshar.htm). Plus incentives had generated more than 300M Robux in creator earnings by Sept 2026. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)
- **Creator Rewards** replaced Premium Payouts in 2025. — [Q3 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm)
- **DevEx:** +8.5%, from $3.50 to $3.80 per 1,000 earned Robux, effective 5 Sept 2025. — [Q3 2025 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509825000326/ex991-q32025shareholderl.htm); [RDC25 DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc25-what-we-announced/3920245). Plus the 42% increase for U.S. 18+ spend in novel R15 games from 8 June 2026 (section 3). — [Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)
- **Cross-experience pass sales disabled:** Roblox's Q2 2026 letter cites "disabling cross-experience game pass sales" as a monetization headwind. — [Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm). Third-party guides date it to ~29-30 May 2026, alongside a Transfers API where only Plus subscribers can send Robux (10-500 per transaction, 10% fee). It hit donation games like PLS DONATE hardest. — [AxeeTech](https://axeetech.com/how-to-transfer-robux-after-the-may-29-update/) (secondary; unverified against the DevForum post)
- **RDC 2026 (Sept 2026):** Roblox Wallet pays eligible U.S. independent creators aged 18+ in real currency each business day (rolling out later in 2026), and a Roblox Card follows in 2027. "Roblox Everywhere" will let games ship as standalone apps on mobile, PC and console. Chrome-based play is due by end of 2026 and offline play is in development, with wider availability targeted for mid-2027. — [PocketGamer.biz, 14 Sept 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)

### Inferences
- **Most important for a new tycoon aimed at kids:** at launch, the game reaches only age-checked 16+ users. It has to earn its way into Kids (5-8) and Select (9-15) through the evaluation (250 highly engaged age-checked plays in 60 days, current docs). That is a real cold-start hurdle for a genre whose core audience skews young. Options: plan for the publishing fee or a Plus/Premium subscription, a Minimal/Mild maturity label, 2FA and ID verification, an Ads Manager Engagement campaign, and possibly expedited review.
- Monetization design should lean on regional pricing, rewarded video (e.g. "watch for 2x income for 15 minutes", which fits idle loops), and frequent small purchases (spend days are an RFY signal). Avoid anything resembling rewarded scrolling, and avoid aggressive pay-to-steal in a kid-facing build.
- The 42% DevEx bonus targets U.S. 18+ spend in "novel", R15-avatar games. A cartoon kid-tycoon will mostly not qualify. A more grown-up tycoon (business or management sim, realistic style, R15) could.
- Weaker monetization among younger cohorts in 2026 lowers expected revenue per hour for a kid-focused tycoon compared with 2025 benchmarks.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 numbers on rewarded video eCPMs or revenue per DAU.
- The exact current Kids and Select fee amount in the docs (vs the DevForum's 1,000 Robux) wasn't stated in the fetched text.
- The current Q3 2026 earnings letter isn't out yet (expected late Oct or early Nov 2026).
- Details of a "commerce API" were not found. Subscriptions inside experiences are mentioned in the DevEx-eligible spend types ("Robux subscriptions"), but no 2026 adoption data was found.

## 6. How saturated is the tycoon genre, how many launch, and what share reach meaningful CCU?

### Takeaway
Tycoon (with Simulator and Obby) is widely described as one of Roblox's most saturated genres. There is **no public, reliable count of tycoon launches per month or of hit rates**. Proxies point the same way: thousands of new games every week, clones that sit in single or double-digit CCU, and a Top-20 that turns over fast but is dominated by a handful of new hits. The one firm structural point is that RFY lets brand-new games reach the top far more than before 2025.

### Cited Findings
- RoLearn's early-2026 genre ranking lists Tycoon as "Highly Saturated" with an opportunity score of 0.30 and high competition density, next to Simulator and Obby. Its guidance calls these "high-risk entry points" for most new developers. These are vendor content, not Roblox data. — [RoLearn: genre saturation](https://rolearn.dev/insights/genre-saturation-opportunity); [RoLearn: genre selection](https://rolearn.dev/guidance/genre-selection-positioning)
- An estimate of ~4,000 new games launched on Roblox every week, with no genre breakdown (vendor estimate). — [RoLearn](https://rolearn.dev/insights/genre-saturation-opportunity) (via search summary)

Airport and firefighter fantasies are described as underserved. All qualitative. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/why-nobody-finds-your-roblox-game-and-the-blue-ocean-strategy-that-fixes-it)

### Inferences
- The genre is crowded at the low end, with templates, kits, AI tools and clones. But the top of the market rewards tycoon-shaped loops more than ever. The bar is execution and differentiation, not the genre label.
- Because similar metadata and place files are deprioritised and RFY scores per-user retention, the "launch a quick clone of the current hit" strategy that worked in mid-2025 is less reliable in late 2026. A differentiated theme plus a proven tycoon/collection/social core is better positioned.
- Realistic outcome distribution (inference, not measured): the vast majority of new tycoons will stay under 100 CCU. Tens to low hundreds of thousands of CCU is "top 50" territory in late 2026 (the GGAID top-50 cutoff was ~21K CCU on 30 Sept 2026, per [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-september-2026/)).
