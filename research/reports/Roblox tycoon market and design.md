# Winning tycoons replace the final statue

The tycoon loop is the most commercially powerful format on Roblox in late 2026, yet the classic version of it is the genre's weakest performer. The three biggest hits of the past eighteen months, Grow a Garden (about 22M peak concurrent players), Steal a Brainrot (25.4M) and Steal An Egg (14.3M in September 2026), are idle tycoons layered with collection, timers and stealing. Meanwhile the 2016–18 dropper tycoons that piled up billions of visits now hold roughly 1–5% of their tracked peaks. The titles that still draw thousands of players years after launch, War Tycoon (13.1k concurrent), Car Dealership Tycoon (10.0k), Theme Park Tycoon 2 (5.9k) and Lumber Tycoon 2 (5.1k), all replaced the "buy the final statue" ending with open-ended creation, collection or recurring conflict, and their developers never stopped shipping updates. Roblox's June 2026 move to a 28-day Recommended For You view turns that design lesson into a distribution rule. Ranking now weighs play-through rate, first-play bounce, play days across Day 1, Days 2–7 and Days 8–28, intentional co-play and spend days, counts only organically acquired players, and deprioritizes look-alike games. Under the hood, a 2026 tycoon should be a server-authoritative value simulation with client-rendered visuals, session-locked saves and idempotent receipts. Its economy should run exponential costs against milestone and rebirth multipliers with capped offline income, and its store should sell convenience, capacity and cosmetics through repeatable developer products, with disclosed odds and per-player policy gating on anything random. Expectations must stay sober: the median Roblox game keeps 10.3% of new players on Day 1 and 0.5% on Day 30, the median DevEx creator earned about $1,500 in the year to June 2026, and a creator keeps roughly 21–27% of each dollar a player spends. For a small team, the best risk-adjusted bet is a co-op management sim with a collection and appointment layer. A collect-and-protect idle game has the higher ceiling but sits in the most saturated and most scrutinized corner of the platform.

## Tycoon-shaped hits rule 2026 while classic droppers keep 1–5% of their peaks

Roblox's fan wiki says the core tycoon is "reminiscent of idle genre, not the business simulation genre." A player claims a base, buys droppers whose bricks ride conveyors to a collector, and spends the cash on buttons that reveal more buttons. The run ends with "a very expensive statue or object", after which players "cannot continue building their base" ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)). That formula built the genre's biggest audiences: **seven pre-2023 tycoons have passed 1.3 billion visits**, led by Car Dealership Tycoon (2.54B) and Super Hero Tycoon (2.49B). But visits measure reach, not health, and the 18 October 2026 snapshot below separates the two sharply.

Rolimons' "all-time" peaks cover only its own tracking window, which starts around 2020, so the 2015–19 peaks of the oldest games are missing. Restaurant Tycoon 2's low ratio most plausibly reflects its audience moving to the developer's own sequel, Restaurant Tycoon 3, which held 13,673 concurrent players on the same day ([Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=7094518649)).

Grouping the table by sub-genre gives a clear verdict. **Classic dropper tycoons have the best record for reach and the worst for retention.** Super Hero Tycoon, Clone Tycoon 2 (798M visits, 167 CCU) and Pizza Factory Tycoon (625M, 84 CCU) average 10–13 minute sessions and keep only small fractions of their peaks ([Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=156428258,180358774)). Creative management sims own the most durable titles (TPT2, Restaurant Tycoon 2, Retail Tycoon 2). Yet the 2020–21 wave of mall, airport, resort and hospital sims peaked at 12k–60k and then collapsed. Combat tycoons are the healthiest sub-genre by current players per title: War Tycoon, Military Tycoon (3,685) and the sixteen-year-old Warfare Tycoon (2,692) are all alive ([Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=2788648141,21449357)). Collection and lifestyle hybrids pair big audiences with the highest approval in the sample. Mansion Tycoon and Giga Mansion Tycoon each hold over 6,000 concurrent players with 96–97% like ratios ([Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=4518160318,4925331346)). Automation sandboxes post the longest sessions (Miner's Haven 51 minutes, Factory Simulator 43) but mostly tiny audiences. Industrialist, with 4,447 CCU and a 95% like ratio, is the exception ([Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=3448264866); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/6769764667)). RNG fads are the least durable of all.

The 2025–26 boom changed the size of the prize without changing that verdict. In Grow a Garden, players plant, harvest, sell and buy rarer seeds; crops keep growing offline and seed stock rotates ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)). In Steal a Brainrot, players buy income-generating characters off a conveyor and steal them from other bases, which owners protect with a temporary shield ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)). Steal An Egg launched in July 2026 and combines stealing with egg hatching, mutations and a Speed treadmill. It peaked at **14.27M concurrent players on 19 September 2026** ([StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-september-2026/); [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10563114921/steal-an-egg)). These are tycoons in all but name. Their peaks were event spikes that faded fast. Steal a Brainrot fell about 99%, from its 25.4M record to roughly 215K by late March 2026 ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/is-the-brainrot-trend-dying-what-ccu-data-from-three-top-games-reveals)). Grow a Garden 2 went from a 976K peak after its June 2026 launch to below the ~21K top-50 cutoff by 30 September ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/games/10200395747/grow-a-garden-2); [StudioKrew](https://studiokrew.com/blog/top-roblox-games-september-2026/)). Roblox's Q2 2026 letter says engagement "shifted more than expected from high-monetizing 2025-vintage viral games to new and evergreen games with lower hourly monetization" ([Roblox Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)).

No game with "Tycoon" in its name appears in the June–October 2026 top-ten snapshots, but a healthy middle tier exists. Restaurant Tycoon 3 (13,673 CCU) and War Tycoon (13,083) sit there alongside two newcomers, Pet Store Tycoon (about 19.8k) and Army Commander Tycoon (about 10.1k), all just under the ~21k level that marked the top 50 on 30 September ([Roblox API: Pet Store Tycoon](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/10282097014/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100); [Roblox API: Army Commander Tycoon](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/10768458048/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100)). **For a new tycoon in 2026, 10,000–20,000 concurrent players is a realistic definition of a hit.** A 10M spike is a lottery ticket.

History explains how the genre split. The uncopylocked Bread Factory Tycoon (2008) set the button standard. Around 2015, free kits from berezaa and Zednov "allowed many users to create their own tycoons with minimal effort," flooding a 2016–17 front page that YouTubers amplified ([Roblox Wiki: Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Tycoon)). Bait-and-switch thumbnails and botting were common until about 2019 ([Fandom: Deceptive advertising](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Deceptive_advertising)). The durable money moved to customizable sims from 2016, to war tycoons and collection hybrids in 2020–21, and to steal-and-collect idle hybrids from 2025. In every wave, the cheap imitators died first.

## Survivors share open-ended goals, living simulations, social showcases and developers who never left

Every durable tycoon replaced the final statue. In Theme Park Tycoon 2, a "Park Rating" driven by guest hunger, thirst, tiredness, restrooms and cleanliness sets future visitor numbers ([Fandom: TPT2](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Player:Den_S/Theme_Park_Tycoon_2)), so a park is never finished, only better. Car Dealership Tycoon turns a dealership into a collection of more than 600 cars. The cars earn money on display and get used in eight race types, a trading hub, seasons, and licensed-brand events such as Zenvo (2024) and an annual Monster Jam Halloween collaboration ([Fandom: CDT](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Foxzie/Car_Dealership_Tycoon)). Its highest tracked peak, 66,726, came in September 2023, five years after launch ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/1554960397)). War Tycoon and Military Tycoon replace completion with recurring fights over capture points. Military Tycoon's progress "does not disappear" when players leave, which removes the server-bound reset that drives churn in classic tycoons ([Fandom: Military Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/InfinityInteractive/Military_Tycoon)). One DevForum reply sums up the alternative: most tycoons are "played only once" unless they break the norm ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-dont-know-why-my-game-is-getting-low-retention/3790969)).

Simulation feedback and social showcase are the second and third shared traits. Retail Tycoon's creator said his favourite part was writing NPC logic, so he could "watch a hundred little artificial people run around doing their own thing" ([Haggie125 interview](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:RBLXDeveloperInterviews/Haggie125:_Retail_Tycoon)). The sequel's product margins, worker automation and multi-factor store rating produce a **34.79-minute average session, the longest of any mainstream business sim in the sample** ([Fandom: Retail Tycoon 2](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Secondhand_Studios/Retail_Tycoon_2); [Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/5865858426)). Restaurant Tycoon 2 lets players visit and work in each other's restaurants, runs on "average smartphones" and supports 20+ languages. It still had about **500,000 daily active users in June 2023**, nearly four years after launch ([IOM Today](https://www.iomtoday.co.im/news/entertainment/game-attracts-more-than-a-billion-plays-619340)). TPT2 puts six parks on a server and lets owners grant build rights to others ([TPT2 Wiki](https://tpt2.fandom.com/wiki/Theme_Park_Tycoon_2)). Lumber Tycoon 2's official fan group awards builder, logistics and trading ranks and runs a weekly Hunger-Games-style event ([Fandom: LT2 Fans](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Lumber_Tycoon_2_Fans)).

The fourth trait, continuity, is the strongest predictor in the data. TPT2 shipped **46 updates in 2025** ([TPT2 Changelog 2025](https://tpt2.fandom.com/wiki/Changelog/2025)). RT2's update log runs unbroken from August 2022 to March 2026 ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/restaurant-tycoon-2-information/356970)). CDT was updated the day before the snapshot. Platform events reward that persistence: Lumber Tycoon 2 set its all-time peak (28,914, per its wiki) on 18 September 2026 during The Hunt: Roblox 20, eleven years after its beta ([LT2 Wiki](https://lumber-tycoon-2.fandom.com/wiki/Lumber_Tycoon_2)). Sequels by the same developer beat new IP. Retail Tycoon 2 reached 340M visits against the original's 218M, and Restaurant Tycoon 2 reached 1.65B against 404M ([Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=111535124,2092410051,331220105,1214576306,7094518649,1934249057,4343544885,3191268194,8724785394,1225802146,10756847038)). Haggie125's new-IP follow-up, Cruise Ship Tycoon, reached only 38.4M ([Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=940371198)). RT2's creator described the sequel as a chance to "build upon what I had already" ([IOM Today](https://www.iomtoday.co.im/news/entertainment/game-attracts-more-than-a-billion-plays-619340)). Depth without a developer fails. Miner's Haven has the longest sessions in the sample, yet it holds 138 concurrent players after its creator left and open-sourced it. Islands fell from 105,800 to 868 after being discontinued ([Fandom: Miner's Haven](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Berezaa_Games%E2%84%A2/Miner%27s_Haven); [Fandom: Islands](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Easy.gg/Islands)).

These traits are necessary, not sufficient. The 2020–21 business-sim imitators had NPC customers, ratings and decoration too, and they still collapsed: Mall Tycoon fell from 59,870 to 381, Airport Tycoon! from 20,950 to 95, and Tropical Resort Tycoon from 25,779 to 205 ([Rolimons: Mall](https://www.rolimons.com/game/5736409216); [Rolimons: Airport](https://www.rolimons.com/game/3440262694); [Rolimons: TRT](https://www.rolimons.com/game/5534174456)). The evidence cannot say exactly why, because **no public D1/D7/D30 figures exist for any named tycoon**. The most plausible reading is that a second-mover copy of a format, without the original's community, depth or update cadence, gives players nothing to come back for. Structure decides whether a tycoon *can* retain; sustained execution decides whether it *does*.

## Kit clones, trend reskins and ninety-minute tycoons fail first

The most common failure is sameness. A widely cited DevForum essay names tycoons among the saturated formats. It warns that developers see only the winners, not the "thousands of simulators that never get players because they're literally all the same" ([DevForum: So your game died...](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/so-your-game-died-a-thread-on-game-design-and-the-market/2644450)). Trend clones fare worst. When "Don't Steal the Bobo" had about 9,500 concurrent players, its clones sat at 31, 11, 7 and 2 ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/why-nobody-finds-your-roblox-game-and-the-blue-ocean-strategy-that-fixes-it)). Bloomberg describes hundreds of Steal a Brainrot imitators that "draw thousands of players before fading" ([Bloomberg via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/03/25/robloxs-hit-game-steal-a-brainrot-battles-its-many-imitators)). Roblox now penalizes copying directly: games with "metadata and place files that closely resemble existing games on Roblox" are "no longer prioritized for recommendations" ([Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)). Free kits carry hidden costs. One circulating kit lists "no saving yet" among its cons ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/novaechelons-tycoon-kit-v1/1423326)). Creator Store models have been found carrying obfuscated scripts that inject Robux purchase prompts and hide them from the game's owner ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-fix-random-gamepass-popups-in-your-game/4580480)).

The second failure is a short, badly paced economy. Reviewers finished one 2025 tycoon in **75–90 minutes** ([DevForum: Feedback on new tycoon](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/feedback-on-new-tycoon/3544642)). That length is fine for Day 1 and worthless for Days 8–28. Another tycoon had both pacing failures at once. Its opening was "Slow as hell. It's torture," with weak early droppers facing "Most items are 15k+." Its rebirths came "way too quickly," and their rewards only slightly raised income ([DevForum: Tycoon Game Feedback](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tycoon-game-feedback/2838875)). One developer's ad-driven spike faded within a month. He blamed an economy where "currency was too easy to make, things were too cheap" and "shop items aren't worth saving up for." Two updates did not stop the decline ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/so-your-game-died-a-thread-on-game-design-and-the-market/2644450)). Standard retention features do not rescue a weak loop. Tropical Incremental reported D1 of 7–10% "despite having daily rewards" ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/low-d1-retention-tropical-incremental/4315335)). Samurai Tycoon had daily rewards, daily quests and multiple rebirths, yet sat below the 30th percentile on D1 and D7 ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-dont-know-why-my-game-is-getting-low-retention/3790969)).

The third failure is buying attention for a game that cannot hold it. Tycoon ads behind generic icons returned **0.1–0.6% click-through rates** ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/why-are-my-ads-and-icon-getting-low-ctr/2411933)). A 50,000-Robux sponsorship produced about 0.7%, and replies advised reaching at least 2% before scaling ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/i-spent-50k-robux-on-sponsorships-and-received-very-poor-results-what-can-i-do-to-improve-my-games-for-the-future/2959734)). Ranking "doesn't count the engagement, monetization, or retention of users first acquired from ads" ([Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)). Bot engagement is excluded, and Roblox began purging bots in August 2026 ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/actioning-against-bot-accounts-on-roblox/4820656)). The 28-day change explicitly targets games that "win attention with exciting thumbnails but don't deliver long-term value" ([Roblox Newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)). Monetization friction makes it worse. Players complain about screen-covering Robux prompts that fire when they accidentally walk over a button, and about cash packs that are "very redundant" in a small tycoon ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tycoon-game-feedback/2838875)).

The fourth failure is technical. Lumber Tycoon 2's developer reportedly said that every duplication glitch he patched revealed "3 more" ([LT2 Wiki: Duplication](https://lumber-tycoon-2.fandom.com/wiki/Duplication)), and fan threads describe the hyperinflation that followed. Community threads trace wiped saves to SetAsync races, saving nil after a failed load, and missing shutdown saves ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/causes-of-player-data-loss/286399)). Server-spawned physics drops are the classic cause of tycoon lag ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tycoon-lag-how-to-fix/387741)). Fads round out the list: Tycoon RNG hit 32,019 concurrent players in July 2024 and has 11 today ([Rolimons](https://www.rolimons.com/game/17601705136)).

## Under the hood, the server simulates value and the client paints the spectacle

The classic architecture is simple. Each player gets a plot tagged with their UserId. Droppers clone a small part with a value attribute every few seconds. Conveyors push it past upgraders that raise the value, and a collector reads the attribute, credits the owner and destroys the part. Touch pads reveal purchases in a branching dependency tree, and rebirth resets everything in exchange for a multiplier ([creation.dev guide](https://www.creation.dev/learn/how-to-make-tycoon-roblox); [creation.dev template](https://www.creation.dev/templates/tycoon-template)). Beginner templates estimate 3–5 hours to build this, and that ease is the trap. Server-spawned physics drops cause the multi-second hangs and brick bursts that plague kit tycoons ([DevForum: Tycoon Lag](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tycoon-lag-how-to-fix/387741)). Roblox's security guide warns that exploiters can take network ownership of unanchored parts and trigger Touched events "at any range or frequency" ([Security tactics](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/security-tactics)). That turns a collector that trusts the part's value into an infinite-money machine.

The robust 2026 pattern reverses the roles: the server simulates *value* and the client renders the *visuals*. The server ticks each owned generator, applies the multipliers its layout implies, and credits pending cash after the known travel time. Clients spawn pooled, non-colliding visual parts, capped at around 200 per client in community practice ([DevForum: Tycoon Lag](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/tycoon-lag-how-to-fix/387741)). With StreamingEnabled, other plots stream in and out, so per-plot client scripts and careful cleanup are needed ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/issue-with-streamingenabled-in-tycoon-game/3884084)).

The rest of the recommended architecture follows from the same principle, and it is engineering recommendation rather than documented Roblox requirement. Purchasables should live in one data table holding ID, price, currency, prerequisites and model, rather than in one script per button. The save file then needs only the purchased IDs and plot-relative placements, which lets the server rebuild any player's base on whichever plot they land. Placement tools should send nothing but an item ID, a grid cell and a rotation for the server to re-validate, and NPC customers in management sims should run as server-side state machines (arrive, queue, order, wait with decaying patience, pay or leave) that clients merely render. Roblox's own rule is absolute: "Assume every piece of data sent from the client has been manipulated," and every remote should be tested by asking what happens at "1,000+" calls per second ([Security tactics](https://create.roblox.com/docs/scripting/security/security-tactics)).

Modern hits add layers on that skeleton. In Steal a Brainrot, each unit earns income per second, and units spawn on a central conveyor on rarity timers: Legendary every 5 minutes, Mythic every 15. Mutations multiply income up to 10x, and completing the index adds +0.5x. A base lock that starts at 30 seconds and grows 10 seconds per rebirth defends the base. Twice-weekly "admin abuse" windows give 10x luck ([BuffGet](https://buffget.com/news/steal-a-brainrot-economy-conveyor-sniping-vs-base-raiding)), though community figures shift between seasons. Restaurant Tycoon 2 rates a restaurant out of five stars across food, service, design, popularity, experience and eco. Higher ratings bring more customers, who are also pickier ([ScreenWise](https://screenwiseapp.com/media/restaurant-tycoon-2-game)). An in-game day lasts about eight real minutes ([Narcosis guide](https://www.narcosis-the-game.com/?p=2002)).

Saving and payments are where tycoons quietly break, and the 2026 answers are settled. ProfileStore (loleris, October 2024) replaces the now-unsupported ProfileService, adding session locking, server-to-server handoff and a 300-second default autosave ([ProfileStore DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/profilestore-save-your-player-data-easy-datastore-module/3190543/); [ProfileService GitHub](https://github.com/MadStudioRoblox/ProfileService)). Roblox's best-practice page says to prefer UpdateAsync whenever a write depends on the current value, retry failed requests with exponential backoff and jitter, keep one key per player under 4 MB, and save on leave, on shutdown and at purchase checkpoints ([Data store best practices](https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud-services/data-stores/best-practices)). BindToClose callbacks get only 30 seconds to finish ([Player data and purchasing](https://create.roblox.com/docs/cloud-services/data-stores/player-data-purchasing)). ProcessReceipt must return PurchaseGranted only after the benefit is granted and saved, with each PurchaseId recorded in the profile so that retries are idempotent ([MarketplaceService](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/MarketplaceService)).

On code structure, Knit was archived in July 2024 because it "cannot fully benefit from types" ([Knit ARCHIVAL.md](https://github.com/Sleitnick/Knit/blob/main/ARCHIVAL.md)). The 2026 norm is plain ModuleScript services with a typed remote layer, built on a Rojo, Rokit, Wally, luau-lsp, Selene and StyLua toolchain ([oflight](https://www.oflight.co.jp/en/columns/luau-developer-toolchain-rojo-selene-wally-2026)). Buffer-based libraries such as Blink and Zap led a community networking benchmark ([DevForum: Packet](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/packet-networking-library/3573907)). Server Authority reached full release on 9 July 2026 as an opt-in mode that blocks injected movement ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/full-release-ship-fair-and-competitive-games-with-server-authority/4727993)). It matters for raiding games, but it does nothing for an economy whose remotes are unvalidated. Roblox publishes no official tycoon template, and no maintained open-source tycoon framework turned up. A small, data-driven build from scratch beats any kit.

| Layer | Table stakes at launch | Differentiators (own one or two) |
|---|---|---|
| Economy core | Server-validated buy, collect and rebirth; value-only simulation with client visuals; formatted big numbers | NPC customer simulation; placement or layout editor; resource logistics |
| Persistence | Session-locked saves, autosave, shutdown save, versioned migrations; idempotent receipts | Trading with atomic swaps inside one server |
| Progression | Visible short, mid and long goals; rebirth that unlocks new content | Collection index with rarities or mutations; a new world every few rebirths |
| Return hooks | Daily and session rewards; Experience Notifications opt-in; codes | Capped offline growth; rotating global restock; weekly online-only drops; live events |
| Social | Leaderboards; friend-join | Shared or adjacent plots; base visits and ratings; co-building; raids with counterplay |
| Monetization | Passes and dev products with correct receipts; Managed Pricing; gift versions | Season pass; rewarded-video boosts; Roblox Plus perks |
| Platform | Mobile-first UI kept out of corner control zones; gamepad pass; low-graphics setting | Shareable showcase moments for Moments clips; localization |

Roblox publishes no tycoon checklist. The table mixes documented platform features with community practice and inference. Two rows deserve emphasis. On mobile, GameAnalytics finds that **55.6% of players use mobile at least some of the time**: 44% play on both PC and mobile, and 11.55% on mobile only ([GameAnalytics 2026](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)). Roblox's UI guidance says to keep buttons out of the bottom corners, where the default controls sit ([Cross-platform UI design](https://create.roblox.com/docs/ui/cross-platform-design)). On return hooks, daily rewards, quests and rebirth are basic hygiene: the anecdotal failures above had all three.

Three engine features are worth adopting from day one. **Rewarded video ads** grant a developer product through ProcessReceipt after a full view, a natural fit for "watch for a 2x boost" offers ([Rewarded video ads](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-ads)). **Experience Notifications** carry "bank full" or "event live" pings that pull players back ([ExperienceNotificationService](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/ExperienceNotificationService)). **The Input Action System** binds one action to touch, gamepad and keyboard at once ([Input Action System](https://create.roblox.com/docs/input/input-action-system)). AI tools can also speed up asset work. Roblox's open-source Studio MCP server lets an LLM insert models and run Luau inside Studio ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-the-open-source-studio-mcp-server/3649365)). One developer reported that players who used 4D generation had 3x the playtime ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/tr/newsroom/2026/02/accelerating-creation-powered-roblox-cube-foundation-model)), though that is a single anecdote.

## The economy runs on exponential curves, and the store should sell time rather than victory

### Pace for the first two minutes and the twenty-eighth day

The standard idle-game economy comes from Anthony Pecorella's Kongregate series. Each generator's next cost is `base × r^owned`, while production grows linearly with the number owned. Exponential cost therefore always overtakes production, and balancing decides when ([Math of Idle Games I](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i)). AdVenture Capitalist's Lemonade Stand uses **r = 1.07** and doubles output at 25 and 50 owned. Those milestone multipliers matter because without them, the newest generator is "nearly always dominant" and older ones become irrelevant. A useful rule follows from the formula: cost doubles every ln2/ln r purchases. At r = 1.07 that is about ten purchases per doubling; at 1.15, about five. Gentle rates suit generators bought many times, and steeper rates suit upgrades bought a few times.

Most Roblox tycoons sell single-purchase buttons instead. For those, community guidance prices each upgrade at **2–5 minutes of current earnings**, treats any wait over 10 minutes as too slow, and plans 30–50 upgrades for a first run ([creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/learn/how-to-make-tycoon-roblox)). Adding up those waits lets a designer set the length of run one on purpose, for example 45–90 minutes, rather than discovering it in playtests.

Rebirth is where long-term pacing lives. Prestige currencies usually scale with a root of earnings. Square-root curves (Realm Grinder, AdVenture Capitalist) need about 4x the previous run's earnings to double the prestige reward. Cookie Clicker's cube root needs about 8x. Egg, Inc.'s 0.14 power needs about 128x, and the game caps offline earnings at **2 hours** ([Math of Idle Games III](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-math-of-idle-games-part-iii)). Roblox convention starts simpler: 2x then 3x multipliers, with a second run about 70% as long as the first ([creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/learn/how-to-make-tycoon-roblox)). Rebirths that only add a multiplier fail. Idle Miner Tycoon found that repeated prestiges "feel the same." It fixed this with new visual mines and limited-time event mines, which produced "a clear inflection point" in revenue ([MobileFreeToPlay](https://mobilefreetoplay.com/gdc-2019-deconstructing-idle-miner-tycoon/)). Every few rebirths should change what the base looks like and what players can buy.

The opening matters even more. GameAnalytics says "D1 is usually won in the first two minutes" ([GameAnalytics 2026](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)). Roblox's onboarding guidance asks for fun within five minutes, low early cost thresholds, starter currency given "without overgiving," and an onboarding that ends with "an intentionally designed moment of joy" ([Roblox Docs: Onboarding](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/onboarding)). Practitioners converge on a first purchase within 30–90 seconds, 3–5 upgrades by minute five and a visible rebirth goal within 15 minutes ([Endsights](https://endsights.com/roblox-tycoon-games)), which are rules of thumb rather than measured optima. Offline income should be a reduced-rate, capped "welcome back" payout that pulls players into the game. Time spent with the game closed earns nothing in Roblox's playtime signals. Controlling inflation comes down to restraint. Stack bonuses additively within a category and multiplicatively only across categories, and tune the curve on the assumption that payers own the 2x pass. On number size, Luau numbers are exact only up to 2^53, about 9 quadrillion, and top out near 1.8e308. Beyond that, libraries such as EternityNum handle values like 1e3003 ([DevForum: EternityNum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-use-eternitynum/2853584)).

### Sell time, capacity and expression rather than victory

Roblox's docs say developer products "generally monetize better, as they can be repeatedly purchased" ([Creator Docs: Monetization](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/get-started/monetization.md)), and live stores bear this out. Grow a Garden sells **zero game passes and more than 1,400 developer products** (median 249 Robux), covering seasonal seeds, pack ladders, eggs and per-crate skips ([Roblox API](https://apis.roblox.com/developer-products/v2/universes/7436755782/developerproducts?limit=100)). Steal a Brainrot sells three passes and 112 developer products, including lucky blocks, spin bundles and dozens of timer skips ([Roblox API](https://apis.roblox.com/developer-products/v2/universes/7709344486/developerproducts?limit=100)).

The durable creative sims monetize lightly and sell capacity, not power. TPT2 offers five passes at 74–374 Robux: collisions off, extra plots and a higher height limit ([Roblox API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/31970568/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100)). Retail Tycoon 2's six cash products have not changed since 2023 ([Roblox API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/2092410051/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100)). Restaurant Tycoon 2's largest unlocks are extra plots, a second floor and a drive-thru ([Roblox API](https://apis.roblox.com/developer-products/v2/universes/1214576306/developerproducts?limit=100)). The table below pulls these catalogs together into a defensible price structure. It is a starting point, not a measured optimum.

The precedents come from the Roblox store APIs for [RT3](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/7094518649/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100), [RT2](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/1214576306/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100), [Pet Simulator 99](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/3317771874/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100), [War Tycoon](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/1526814825/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100), [Military Tycoon](https://apis.roblox.com/developer-products/v2/universes/2788648141/developerproducts?limit=100), [Steal a Brainrot](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/7709344486/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100) and [Car Dealership Tycoon](https://apis.roblox.com/developer-products/v2/universes/605887098/developerproducts?limit=100). They show default prices; regional pricing lowers what many players actually see.

Where to draw the line is clearest in feedback from developers. The top DevForum reply on one tycoon's monetization plan said to avoid passes that directly boost income, "ESPECIALLY 2x cash," because it "will easily drive away potential players." It also advised making any premium currency earnable without paying ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/monetization-ideas-for-my-tycoon-game/3048249)). The advice matters most in PvP. War Tycoon sells 1,999-Robux jets and submarines and a "Double HP Armor" pass ([Roblox API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/1526814825/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100)). Polygon criticized Steal a Brainrot because "the best items are only available for cash" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot)). Both games still earn heavily, so backlash and revenue can coexist, but the reputational and regulatory cost falls hardest on games with young audiences. The safer things to sell are convenience, capacity, creative tools, cosmetics and gifts.

The economics are thinner than headline payouts suggest. Creators receive 70% of the Robux price ([Creator Docs: Roblox Plus](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/roblox-plus)). DevEx has paid **$0.0038 per Robux** since 5 September 2025, or $0.0054 on qualifying purchases by age-verified U.S. adults ([Creator Docs: DevEx](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-exchange)). Players pay roughly $0.010–0.0125 per Robux, so a creator nets about 21–27% of each dollar a player spends. On qualifying adult spend the share rises to about 38%, consistent with Roblox's stated increase from 26.6% to 37.8% ([Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/roblox-to-raise-developer-exchange-rate-for-games-aimed-at-18-plus-players-as-adult-cohort-surges/)).

Few players pay at all. Only **3.80% of players in GameAnalytics' sample spent anything**, and median spend per paying player (ARPPU) was $0.70 ([GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/gameanalytics-key-roblox-and-roblox)). As a hypothetical, a tycoon with 10,000 daily users averaging 10 Robux of spend per user per day would net about $8,000 a month; at 3 Robux, about $2,400. Payouts are extremely concentrated. The top 1,000 creators averaged $1.4M in the year to June 2026, while the median DevEx creator earned about $1,500 ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/roblox/robloxs-top-creators-average-65-7-million-a-year-but-most-make-far-less-3408675/)). Spending is also softening at the platform level. Q2 2026 per-hour monetization fell "especially among younger U.S. and Canadian users," and Roblox guided Q3 bookings down 14–18% year over year ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)).

Three platform programs shape a new tycoon's revenue beyond its own store. **Creator Rewards** pays 5 Robux per "Active Spender" who plays at least 10 minutes in a day, but only if the game is among the first three that user launches that day, plus 35% of a new or returning user's first $100 of purchases ([DevForum: Creator Rewards](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628)), so it pays directly for daily returns. **Managed Pricing**, live since 15 June 2026, sets regional prices between 30% and 100% of the default and runs price tests; Roblox reports 43–52% more pass purchases in discounted regions and about 4% higher earnings from price tests, often achieved by lowering prices ([DevForum: Managed Pricing](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/managed-pricing-one-system-for-better-pricing-and-earnings-growth/4684738)). **Roblox Plus** replaced Premium on 30 April 2026, which makes Premium-only perks obsolete ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/roblox/plus-subscription)); Plus subscribers get free private servers, and creators earn up to 100 Robux per subscriber who spends an hour in one ([Creator Docs: Roblox Plus](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/roblox-plus)).

### Policy makes randomness and urgency the riskiest items in the store

Any chance-based purchase now carries compliance work. Roblox's paid random items policy reaches well beyond loot boxes: it covers eggs, chests and wheels, random-duration potions, luck boosts and pity systems, indirect purchases such as keys and spin tickets, and anything bought with currency that was itself bought with Robux. Games must show "all possible outcomes and the actual numerical odds" before a player spends, and for players where PolicyService's `ArePaidRandomItemsRestricted` returns true, they must switch to a free earnable path, a disclosed fixed order, direct sale at expected value, or removal ([Creator Docs: Paid random items](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)). In Brazil, the Digital ECA law bars under-18s from paid random mechanics, and games that ignore the PolicyService API are reportedly unplayable there for minors ([Roblox Help](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/cs/articles/48706630819476-Brazil-Digital-ECA-Updates)). Simulated gambling is banned outright ([Creator Docs: Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)). Rewarded video ads carry their own rules: they must be opt-in, shown only to users aged 13 and older and never required to progress, the reward must be a non-random developer product worth about 3–10 Robux, and the game needs 2,000+ monthly unique visitors to qualify ([Creator Docs: Rewarded video ads](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/rewarded-video-ads)).

Reaching children is now gated. Roblox Kids (ages 5–8) and Select (ages 9–15) launched globally on 16 June 2026. New games are visible only to age-checked users 16 and older until they record **250 unique plays from highly engaged, age-checked players within 60 days** and pass a review. Creators need ID verification, two-step verification, and either a refundable fee or a Plus subscription. An expedited review costs a refundable 50,000 Robux ([Creator Docs: Kids and Select](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select)). The June DevForum launch post gave different numbers (500 highly engaged players, and 100,000 Robux for expedited review), so check the live docs before planning ([DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox-kids-and-select-global-launch-upcoming-updates-to-eligibility-ads-manager-and-expedited-review/4685717)).

Kids accounts see only Minimal or Mild content. A Moderate-rated game, such as a violent war tycoon, reaches Select accounts but not Kids accounts ([Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)). Roblox has also shown it acts quickly against exploitative engagement. In August 2026 it pulled Steal An Egg for several hours, while the game had 500K+ concurrent players, over a treadmill "Reels" feed. It then banned games that "reward ongoing media feed consumption" from Kids and Select ([eGamers](https://egamers.io/roblox-bans-games-that-pay-kids-to-keep-scrolling-in-wake-of-steal-an-egg-uproar/)). Roblox also disabled cross-experience game pass sales around late May 2026, a change its Q2 letter lists among bookings headwinds and that hit donation games hardest ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm); [AxeeTech](https://axeetech.com/how-to-transfer-robux-after-the-may-29-update/)).

External regulators point the same way. The UK ICO's Children's Code says children "should not be manipulated into quick or rash purchases through countdown or expiring offers" and that purchases should not appear necessary to progress ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-and-services/audits/data-protection-audit-framework/toolkits/age-appropriate-design/nudge-techniques/)). The EU's consumer-protection network launched coordinated action against nine unnamed game companies on 15 October 2026 over loot boxes and "direct exhortations to children," and a draft Digital Fairness Act is expected in November ([Lewis Silkin](https://www.lewissilkin.com/insights/2026/10/15/eu-consumer-protection-network-launches-action-involving-nine-video-games-compani-102o5do)). In the US, the FTC's $20M Genshin Impact settlement restricted loot-box sales to under-16s ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/hoyoverse-agrees-to-20-million-settlement-with-ftc-over-loot-boxes)). The most future-proof tycoon store is therefore deterministic by default: it sells items directly, brings seasonal content back instead of running countdowns, shows prices clearly in real-money terms, and uses paid randomness only where it is disclosed and gated.

## Roblox's 28-day algorithm pays for return days, not spikes

The Home page drives over 90% of platform traffic, and Discovery serves more than 10.5 billion impressions a day ([DevForum: 2025 Discovery Roadmap](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/roblox%E2%80%99s-2025-discovery-roadmap-and-ways-to-reach-and-engage-your-audience/3557533)). The Recommended For You (RFY) sort is effectively the market. On 15 June 2026 Roblox widened RFY from a 7-day to a **28-day view**, removed qualified play-through rate, and introduced play-through rate and first-play bounce rate ([DevForum: RFY improvements](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)). It accepted the revenue cost knowingly: the change "intentionally provides more impressions for highly retentive games at the expense of near term monetization" ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)). The table lists the current signals from the Creator Hub discovery page ([Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)).

| Signal | Roblox's ranking | Measurement window | What it rewards in a tycoon |
|---|---|---|---|
| Play-through rate (PTR) | Most important | Per Home impression | An honest, readable thumbnail and title |
| First-play bounce rate | Most important (counts against you) | Exits under 60 s and at 61–180 s | Visible income and a first purchase within the first minute |
| Play days per user | Most important | D1, D2–7, D8–28 | Offline growth, restocks, weekly drops, goals left unfinished |
| Playtime per user | Most important; capped at 60 min per user per day | D1, D2–7, D8–28 | Full sessions that need not exceed an hour |
| Intentional co-play days | Important | D1, D2–7, D8–28 | Friend joins, invites, private servers, shared plots |
| Qualified play sessions | Important | D1, D2–7, D8–28 | Repeated, meaningful sessions |
| Spend days per user | Important | D1, D2–7, D8–28 | Cheap, frequent purchases, not only big spenders |
| Robux spent per user | Important | D1, D2–7, D8–28 | Overall monetization |

Several rules shape how these signals behave. They are per-user averages "so smaller games are not disadvantaged," and they count only users who arrived organically through RFY, so players acquired through ads add nothing and Creator Analytics benchmarks do not affect ranking. Look-alike metadata and place files are deprioritized, "Metadata that implies any type of monetary reward is not prioritized," and metadata that does not match the gameplay gets less exposure ([Creator Docs: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)). Gameplay videos on RFY must be authentic gameplay, not cinematic trailers. Roblox reports up to 39% more RFY playtime for games that have one. Standout Games, a hand-curated row, accepts nominations through a form ([DevForum: Improving Home](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571)). At RDC 2026, Roblox said ranking will become age-aware, recommending shorter games to younger players and deeper, return-visit games to older ones. It is also testing a "direct growth" signal for bringing brand-new players to the platform, and from October, items shown in Moments clips can be bought ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-unveils-new-play-creation-and-monetisation-tools-at-rdc-2026/)).

The new system churns the charts faster. After RFY went global in March 2025, brand-new games' share of Top-20 visits rose from **1.3% to 60.7%**. The median time a game stays in the Top 20 fell from 14 months to 7 ([Dubit](https://dubit.io/blog/roblox-top-20-rfy)). The top ten games now take about 20% of hours, down from about 30% three years earlier, and hours outside the top ten grew 25% year over year ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)). A new tycoon can break through more easily than before 2025, but it has to keep earning its place every month.

Benchmarks keep targets honest. GameAnalytics' 2026 report covers 500+ games with over 1M monthly active users. In that sample, the **median game has 10.3% D1, 1.6% D7 and 0.5% D30 retention; the top 1% reach 22.2%, 9.1% and 4.7%**. The median session is 9.8 minutes, and the median game gets 1.56 sessions per player per day ([GameAnalytics 2026](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2026-roblox-report)). The report calls the widely shared "20% D1 is good, 40% is excellent" charts inflated and gives 12.9–15.9% as the D1 target range. Its 2025 data shows longer sessions tracking better retention: median D1 was 4.31% for games averaging 0–3 minute sessions and 11.46% for 19–24 minutes ([GameAnalytics 2025](https://www.gameanalytics.com/reports/2025-roblox-report)). Neither report breaks results out by genre. A reasonable target for a good tycoon is therefore D1 of 13–16%, D7 of 2.5–5%, D30 of 1–1.5%, and sessions of 12–15+ minutes. These figures are inferred from the percentile ranges, not drawn from tycoon data. One gap matters: Roblox has not said how brand-new games get tested under 28-day windows. When developers asked, the June announcement thread did not answer ([DevForum: RFY improvements](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575)).

The practical implications are inferences from these signals rather than Roblox guidance, but they are consistent. A tycoon should be designed for **a daily 10-minute return rather than an AFK marathon**: play days and Creator Rewards both pay for daily returns, playtime counts only up to 60 minutes a day, and Roblox warns that games with "unusually long sessions may see reduced earnings" under Creator Rewards ([DevForum: Creator Rewards](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/introducing-creator-rewards-earn-more-by-growing-the-community/3777628)). Co-play should work without chat, because age checks left on-platform communication below pre-check levels ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)); shared plots, invites and joint goals generate co-play days that talk-dependent features no longer can. Some items should be cheap enough to buy repeatedly so that spend days accumulate, and paid ads should be treated as an instrument for measuring PTR and D1, not as a way to lift rank. Finally, localization is a growth feature: in Q2 2026, DAU grew 67% in Japan and 64% in India against 6% in the U.S. and Canada ([Q2 2026 letter](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)), and Restaurant Tycoon 2 already ships in 20+ languages.

## Four concept directions trade ceiling against durability

The evidence describes two business models. A "viral live-ops machine" chases event spikes with collection, timers and weekly drops. A "durable niche" grows a few thousand loyal players through creativity or conflict and keeps them for years. The four directions below span that range. Each is built from mechanics with a proven lineage and adds one twist aimed at the 2026 ranking signals.

The themes are illustrative. Choose any theme by testing the play-through rate of two or three honest thumbnails with small ad budgets. The evidence argues against food, fishing and brainrot/steal reskins, which are dominated or saturated. RoWatcher calls fishing a "red ocean" and lists airport and firefighter fantasies as underserved ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/why-nobody-finds-your-roblox-game-and-the-blue-ocean-strategy-that-fixes-it)). Airport Tycoon 2 did launch in August 2026, with 1.8M visits so far ([Roblox API](https://games.roblox.com/v1/games?universeIds=10756847038)).

The build scopes are rough estimates for a team of two to four with one strong scripter. The sources offer only three reference points: a beginner tycoon template takes 3–5 hours ([creation.dev](https://www.creation.dev/templates/tycoon-template)), Grow a Garden's first version reportedly took about three days ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)), and Restaurant Tycoon 2 took about six months ([IOM Today](https://www.iomtoday.co.im/news/entertainment/game-attracts-more-than-a-billion-plays-619340)).

| | A. Co-op venue sim with collection | B. Collect-and-protect idle | C. Co-op automation sandbox | D. Co-op mission tycoon (PvE) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proven lineage | TPT2, RT2/RT3, Retail Tycoon 2, Pet Store Tycoon | Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot, Steal An Egg, Sell Lemons | Lumber Tycoon 2, Industrialist, Miner's Haven | War Tycoon, Military Tycoon, Car Dealership Tycoon |
| Lineage CCU today | TPT2 5.9k; RT3 13.7k; Pet Store ~19.8k | Steal An Egg ~1.3M; Sell Lemons ~135k (June); trend clones often in double digits | LT2 5.1k; Industrialist 4.4k; Miner's Haven 138 | War Tycoon 13.1k; Army Commander ~10.1k; Military 3.7k |

### Concept A: a co-op venue sim with a collection layer (recommended)

The core loop puts four to six adjacent plots on each server. Players place attractions and fixtures on a grid and serve NPC guests whose needs (comfort, queue time, cleanliness, novelty) roll up into a multi-factor star rating, as in TPT2 and RT2; the rating drives foot traffic and the prices guests will accept, hired staff automate service, and income buys land, floors and new fixture tiers. A collection layer sits on top: rare fixtures, exhibits or signature items in fixed, disclosed tiers arrive through a rotating global shop and through milestones, each with a visible rating bonus, so the venue doubles as a trophy case. Appointment mechanics borrowed from the live-ops hits supply the reasons to return: a capped offline revenue payout, a restock every few hours, a weekly themed drop and a short daily rush-hour event, a mechanic Restaurant Tycoon 3 already monetizes with a "Start Rush Hour" product ([RT3 Wiki: Shop](https://rt3.fandom.com/wiki/Shop); [Roblox API](https://apis.roblox.com/game-passes/v1/universes/7094518649/game-passes?passView=Full&pageSize=100)). Friends can co-build, staff each other's venues and rate their neighbours.

It could retain because it stacks the two strongest longevity patterns on record, open-ended expression with simulation feedback (TPT2, RT2, Retail Tycoon 2) and collection (CDT, Pet Simulator), on a social layer that produces intentional co-play days without relying on chat. Pet Store Tycoon (~19.8k CCU) and RT3 (13.7k) show that a management tycoon can still climb to the edge of the top 50 in 2026. Its depth suits the coming age-aware ranking for older players, and it monetizes through the capacity and creative passes that TPT2 and RT2 show players accept. An R15, less cartoonish art direction could also qualify U.S. adult spend for the higher DevEx rate, which Roblox ties to "novel" games ([Roblox newsroom](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)), though how strictly that word is applied is unclear.

The key risk is that the 2020–21 business-sim wave already proved structure alone is not enough, so the theme must be fresh and the updates relentless. This is also the most engineering-heavy direction, with placement validation, NPC state machines, the rating model, plot streaming and phone performance all on the critical path, and it earns less per hour than steal games. A rough scope is 4–6 months to a soft launch with grid placement, 25–40 fixtures, three staff roles, the rating model, four-to-six-plot servers, saves and receipts, followed by updates every two to four weeks, the cadence Roblox suggests ([Roblox Docs: Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)), and a weekly shop rotation in between.

### Concept B: a collect-and-protect idle tycoon (highest ceiling, most crowded field)

The core loop has players buy rarity-tiered units from a shared conveyor or rotating restock and place them in their base, where they earn per second and keep maturing while the player is offline, up to a cap. Units combine or mutate into multipliers, an index rewards completion, and rebirth opens a new zone with new unit families. Raids exist but carry protections designed for young players: stealing happens only during scheduled server-wide raid hours or against bases whose owners opted in, base locks are earned through play and never sold, offline bases are protected, and a return-on-hit rule makes a struck thief drop the loot. Twice-weekly live events with luck boosts and weekly content drops supply the spikes.

It could retain because this is the mechanic stack behind the three largest Roblox games ever. Appointment play through restocks, offline growth and weekly online-only items produces play days in the Day 2–7 and Day 8–28 windows and matches Creator Rewards' daily 10-minute pattern, while stealing generates the social stories Player.One credits for Steal An Egg's rise ([Player.One](https://www.player.one/robloxs-steal-egg-beating-some-platforms-biggest-games-164086)). Sell Lemons shows that a classic idle tycoon with prestige resets and fruit evolutions can reach about 135K concurrent players without any stealing at all ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/the-most-popular-roblox-games-right-now-ranked-by-live-players); [AllThings.How](https://allthings.how/sell-lemons-on-roblox-how-to-farm-cash-rebirth-and-evolve/)).

The risks are the heaviest of the four. This is the most copied format on the platform at the moment Roblox began deprioritizing look-alikes, incumbents hold 100K to more than 1M players, eggs and lucky blocks require odds disclosure and PolicyService gating, and stealing designs already attract pay-to-win criticism and viral clips of distressed children. Peaks decay 90–99%, and the content treadmill is brutal: BIG Games moved Pet Simulator 99 from weekly to bi-weekly updates after 60 weeks ([BIG Games](https://www.biggames.io/post/pet-simulator-99-update-69)), Grow a Garden pivoted after "disappointing updates" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/robloxs-top-experience-grow-a-garden-is-shifting-into-a-new-era-and-promises-deeper-farming-after-a-series-of-disappointing-updates-i-hope-the-community-can-see-this-change-as-a-positive-attempt-to-keep-the-game-fresh-and-enjoyable-for-everyone/)), and Adopt Me's weekly schedule "resulted in crunch" ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/how-these-developers-hope-to-build-a-100-person-game-studio-inside-roblox/)). The core is the smallest build of the four, roughly 6–10 weeks to a polished MVP, but the real budget is a permanent content pipeline of at least one artist and one scripter shipping new units and events every week.

### Concept C: a co-op automation sandbox for teens and adults (the depth bet)

The core loop starts with players extracting resources by hand and then by machine, routing them by conveyor, rail or vehicle through processors that turn them into higher-value goods, selling those goods against contracts, researching new machines and expanding into new biomes. Friends share a factory and can specialize in mining, logistics or sales, while weekly contracts and seasonal biomes create dated reasons to return. The server simulates item flow as numbers and clients render the belts, the same value-versus-visuals split that keeps droppers cheap.

It could retain because the lineage is unusually durable for its size: Lumber Tycoon 2 is still setting records eleven years in, Industrialist holds 4,447 concurrent players at a 95% like ratio, and automation games post 43–51 minute sessions. Roblox's coming age-aware ranking will send deeper, return-visit games to older players, and 16+ is exactly the audience a new game launches to by default. Systems depth supplies a natural "never finished" axis, and the game can monetize lightly through capacity such as land, machine slots, blueprint storage and cosmetics.

The risks are a niche ceiling (Miner's Haven holds 138 players and Factory Simulator 216), a complex first session that collides with the two-minute D1 rule and loses younger players, a 60-minute daily playtime cap that gives marathon sessions no extra ranking credit, part counts that strain phones, and duplication exploits if trading is added. A rough scope is 4–7 months, dominated by placement, the item-flow simulation, the research tree and performance work.

### Concept D: a co-op mission tycoon with war-tycoon structure and PvE stakes

The core loop gives each player a base that earns passively and funds a garage of collectible vehicles and gear; an illustrative theme is a fire-and-rescue headquarters, one of the fantasies RoWatcher lists as underserved. A shared map spawns AI incidents or objectives that pay out on timed cycles, much as Military Tycoon's capture points pay 14,000 cash roughly every 90 seconds ([Fandom: Military Tycoon](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/InfinityInteractive/Military_Tycoon)). Players drive out alone or in squads while hired NPC crews handle small calls during idle time, rebirth unlocks new districts and vehicle classes, and vehicles are customized and shown off as in Car Dealership Tycoon.

It could retain because war tycoons are the healthiest sub-genre by current players per title, Army Commander Tycoon shows a newcomer can still reach about 10k concurrent players, and Car Dealership Tycoon shows a vehicle collection can sustain a game for most of a decade. Keeping the stakes PvE preserves a Minimal or Mild rating that keeps Kids and Select within reach, removes the spawn-camping and pay-to-win backlash PvP tycoons attract ([DevForum: anti-spawn killing](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-does-one-make-an-effective-anti-spawn-killing-system/3266905)), and turns squads into a steady source of co-play days.

The central risk is that the concept rests on an untested hypothesis, namely that cooperative objectives can replace the PvP tension that most plausibly keeps war tycoons alive. Its scope is the largest of the four, spanning vehicles, AI, a shared map and missions, and Car Dealership Tycoon already offers police and fire jobs. A straight PvP version has stronger evidence behind it but brings griefing, a Moderate rating that locks out Kids accounts, and well-funded incumbents. A rough scope is 5–8 months to a soft launch.

### How to choose

Concept A gives a small team the best odds of a multi-year business with modest, stable income, and bolting B's appointment layer onto it (restocks, capped offline growth, weekly drops) borrows the viral hits' return mechanics without their saturation or policy exposure. B suits a team that can commit to weekly content indefinitely and accepts lottery-like odds; C suits a team that loves systems and wants an older audience; D has the most open space in the market and the most uncertainty. Whatever the choice, the evidence supports the same validation sequence: test the play-through rate of honest thumbnails before committing, build a four-to-six-week vertical slice, soft-launch it to the default 16+ audience, measure first-play bounce and D1 against the 13–16% target, and iterate on onboarding before spending on ads or pursuing Kids and Select eligibility.

## Conclusion

The genre's dividing line has moved from mechanics to time horizon. In 2016 the question was whether a tycoon could reach the front page; in 2026 it is whether Day 20 offers something Day 1 did not. Roblox's ranking, its payouts and outside regulators now push in the same direction, with play days counted through Day 28, Creator Rewards paying for daily 10-minute visits, a higher DevEx rate for adult spending, and regulators targeting countdowns and paid randomness. Together they favour deeper, social, honestly monetized games for slightly older players, which means the design that keeps a tycoon compliant is also the one the algorithm rewards. The steal-and-collect boom does not contradict this: its winners retained players through appointments and collection, and its imitators were exactly the look-alike, short-horizon games the new ranking now filters out.

The practical lesson is to treat the first version as the first of a series. The most consistent success pattern in the record is a developer iterating on their own proven concept, with Retail Tycoon 2 and Restaurant Tycoon 2 drawing about 1.5x and 4x their originals' visits, and the most consistent failure is abandonment. A small team should therefore pick the concept it can update every two to four weeks for years, and design its data and IP so that a sequel can inherit players' collections, as Pet Simulator did when players carried pets from PSX into PS99 ([RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/inside-big-games-how-pet-simulator-became-a-100m-roblox-empire)). In a market where the median game keeps half a percent of its players for a month, the hardest asset to copy is an audience that trusts the developer to keep shipping.
