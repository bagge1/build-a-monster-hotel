## Title

**Build A Monster Hotel!** (August's, 2026-10-23)

## Description

Claude's draft, 2026-10-23: 989 characters, under Roblox's 1000. The Halloween
line comes out after 3 November (`Events.luau`, the season's end). "Every few
minutes" rather than a number, so a change to the bus's interval does not date
it. Rewrite it when a hotel or a feature it names changes.

```
🦇 Build a hotel for monsters! The rarest monsters only check in to the best hotel on the street.

Every few minutes the Night Bus rolls into Creepy Lane full of guests: Slimes, Skeletons, Pumpkin Heads, Vampire Counts and rarer things still. Up to ten hotels share the street, and every guest picks the one that suits them best. Run out and pitch yours!

🛏️ BUILD your way: walls, doors, rooms, floors, stairs, windows and spooky decor.
⭐ RAISE your star rating with happy guests, clean rooms and a busy front desk.
👑 TAKE VIP contracts and build the perfect room for a picky guest.
👻 BEFRIEND guests so they move in, level up and earn while you're away.
📖 COLLECT every species, rare mutation and hidden decor in the Monster Book.
🎃 DEFEND the street together on Hunter Night: shout Boo! and set traps.
❄️ REBIRTH to open Frostfang Lodge and Bayou Spa, with new monsters.

🍬 HALLOWEEN is on until 3 November: collect Candy and find Pumpkin mutations!

Cute, cozy and just a little spooky.
```
